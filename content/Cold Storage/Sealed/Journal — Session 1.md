Four of them, and I'll give you the names, because the names are the one thing I came away with tonight that I'm sure of.

[[Trip]] is the Tremere — a warlock, though he let the rest of us arrive at it ourselves rather than say it, which I respected and also filed away. He runs a clinic with rooms above it and a clientele that has learned not to ask, and he carries the flat calm of a man who has cleaned up worse than tonight and expects to again; his ghoul, [[Dutch]], struck me as a genuinely decent person, which is its own small misfortune given the night Dutch was about to have. Trip's composure has a ceiling. We found it.

[[Mitch]] is Gangrel, and the reason he is in this at all is a brother — [[Carter]], a trapeze artist, mortal, the soft place someone located and leaned on. Whoever has the rest of us by the throat has Carter too. Mitch hasn't understood yet what he is; I watched him not understand it, the way you watch someone stand in a doorway they don't know is a doorway, and I remembered how long it took me, and I did not envy him the rest of the learning.[^r1]

[[Spider]] is Nosferatu, sent down by someone above him to handle the same [[Walter Nash|Nash]] we are all apparently here to handle, and he has some history with [[Darkhour Security]] that nobody explained and he didn't volunteer. He is quiet. He asked Trip, almost in passing, whether there was an easier way to feed — the kind of thing a person only says aloud once he's been losing the private version of that argument for a while.

[[Collin]] is the one who doesn't fit. Nobody blackmailed him; he was already hunting Nash on his own and walked into the four of us sideways. He talks to people by handwritten note, he ran our whole problem through his own people and bounced off [[Darkhour Security|Darkhour]]'s walls, and he spent the meeting watching the rest of us the exact way I was watching him, which is the only reason I trust him at all.

The footage is the worst of it. Collin swept Carter's apartment and turned up a camera and months behind it. A woman sets the equipment up and then crawls back into a wall — the timestamps are daylight, so she's mortal, and she has the loose, unbothered movements of someone who doesn't know she's doing any of it. I would like very much to know who holds her leash.[^rcl] On the same reel: Nash either feeding or being fed upon, I genuinely couldn't tell from the angle, and a second man with a [[Honored Drinks]] logo on the back of his jacket — tall, white, blond, tattoos up the neck. Later that night, that same description, almost certainly that same man, broke Carter's arm trying to get Mitch's address out of him. Carter didn't give it. They made Dutch stand and watch, because the watching was the message.

Three threads to pull, and we pick one before we rise: [[Darkhour Security|Darkhour]], which is yours; [[Honored Drinks]], which is new; and [[verumtenebris.org|verumtenebris]], which is yours too, dead-ended, and still ours.

The clinic. You'll hear the word "frenzy" and picture something cleaner than what happened. Dutch brought Mitch the news about Carter, and Mitch's Beast decided on the spot that Dutch had been present and failed to stop it, so Dutch was the one it went for. Trip improvised — money, Dominate, sedatives — and Dutch is alive, barely, and there are ten people in that clinic who saw something they shouldn't have. Trip called it a problem deferred rather than solved, and I think he knew exactly how right he was while he was saying it.

Mitch came to the meeting an hour late in Trip's clothes because his own weren't wearable. He didn't speak. Trip wouldn't let him. Whatever is supposed to grow between those two will have to climb over that first, and I don't know yet that it will.

Four people aimed at one problem by four separate powers, plus one who aimed himself. I don't know yet whether the shape means anything. I'm noting it in case.

I watched Mitch try not to think about his brother and fail at it, and I thought about Holly and Joy, and I thought about you, and I kept my face still. You know I did. I'm writing it down anyway.[^r2]

Give the girls my love in whatever form you can manage without lying to them. I'll find you before I sleep.[^r3]
