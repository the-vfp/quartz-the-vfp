Four of them. I'll give you names.

**[[Trip]] (Tremere).** Runs a private clinic with living quarters above. Reads competent; his ghoul [[Dutch]] is a decent man; his composure has a ceiling that got found tonight. More below.

**[[Mitch]] (Gangrel).** Has a mortal brother — [[Carter]], trapeze performer — who is the reason he's here. Whoever has us by the throat has Carter. Mitch hasn't figured out what he is yet. He will.

**[[Spider]] (Nosferatu).** Sent by a superior to deal with [[Walter Nash]]. Has some prior with [[Darkhour Security|Darkhour]] that no one's explained. Quiet. Asked Trip in passing about blood bag hookups, which tells me he isn't eating well.

**[[Collin]] (Brujah).** The odd one. Wasn't blackmailed — was investigating Nash independently and walked into the rest of us sideways. Communicates only by handwritten note at his office. Ran Darkhour through his people and bounced off their online security. Watching him listen to four other people talk about being extorted was its own information.

**The footage.** Collin swept Carter's apartment and found a camera. Months of material. A woman I'll call the crawlspace lady sets up the equipment and then crawls back into a wall. Daylight timestamps — she's mortal and apparently doesn't know she's doing it. I want to know who handles her.

Same footage: Nash feeding, or being fed on — I couldn't tell from the angle. The other man wore a jacket branded with a bar called [[Honored Drinks]] on the back. Tall, white, blonde, neck tattoos. Later that night the same description, probably the same man, broke Carter's arm trying to get Mitch's address out of him. Carter didn't talk. Dutch was made to watch — that was the price of the message.

Three leads: Darkhour (your trail), Honored Drinks (new), [[verumtenebris.org|verumtenebris]] (your trail, dead-ended but still ours). We'll pick one before we rise.

**The clinic.** Mitch frenzied at Trip's clinic when Dutch brought the news about Carter. The Beast decided Dutch was guilty for not protecting Carter and went for him. Trip improvised. Dutch is alive — barely — and the clinic has ten witnesses Trip managed with money, Dominate, and sedatives. He called it a problem deferred. He's right.

Mitch arrived at the meeting an hour late wearing Trip's clothes because his own were unusable. He didn't speak; Trip wouldn't let him. The distance between them is going to be a problem before it's a partnership.

**What I'm making of it.** Four people sent at the same problem by four different powers, plus one who walked in on his own. I don't know if the shape matters. Noting it in case.

Carter is the clearest version of the shape we're in. I watched Mitch try not to think about his brother and fail, and I thought about the girls and I thought about you, and I kept my face still. You know I did. Writing it down anyway.

Give [[Holly Marte|Holly]] and [[Joy Marte|Joy]] my love in whatever form you can manage without lying. I'll find you before I sleep.
