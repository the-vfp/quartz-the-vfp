[[Raphael]] had a knife when I got home.

Not using it. Not even holding it correctly — the santoku from the block, the one he keeps sharp because he's the one who cooks. Sitting on the bed with his phone in one hand and the knife in the other and he was just _gone_. I said his name three times before he looked at me.

He came back fast, though. That's the thing about him. He hits the wall and then he comes back and he starts working.

Kids are fine. We checked together. Both asleep.

**What we know:**

- Voicemail on [[Raphael]]'s personal cell. Voice changer, but female. I listened to it twice. Phrasing was rehearsed — not scripted-read, rehearsed. She's said this before or practiced saying it.
- Demand: monthly wire, 25% of [[Bridgepoint]]'s income. Not a lump sum. Sustained. Whoever this is wants a tap, not a payout.
- Leverage: video of me feeding. 7-Eleven bathroom, maybe five weeks ago. Bad hunt night. Cellphone footage, not security camera. Somebody was there or nearby. Somebody followed me or knew where I'd end up.
- The email came to [[Raphael]]'s work address. That's not public information. That took effort.
- [[Raphael]] ran the banking details tonight while I made calls. Trail leads to a local outfit called [[Darkhour Security]]. Small operation. Could be the principal or a cutout — he's leaning cutout, I'm not sure yet. We need more before we commit to either read.

**What we're doing:** Safehouse plan is active. We built it two years ago and updated it in January, so the bones are solid. [[Raphael]] and the girls leave tomorrow night. We agreed on what to tell them: enough to take it seriously, not enough to weaponize against them. No lies. No minimizing. That line is one we've walked before and we're both getting too practiced at it.

Wire transfer is prepped as a contingency. If we need to buy time, we buy time. We agreed on that without arguing about it, which tells me we're both more scared than we're performing right now.

There's a group of [[Camarilla]] neonates already investigating Darkhour for their own reasons. I don't know what those reasons are yet and neither of us loves that. But it's a way in. Easier to move alongside people already pointed in the right direction than to start cold and risk crossing their operation. [[Raphael]]'s going to keep working the financial side from the safehouse. I'll feed him what I get from the neonates. We'll compare notes the way we always do.

**What I haven't said to him:** I know what that video looked like. I remember that night. I was hungry and sloppy and there was nothing controlled about it, and now that's in his head permanently because someone put it there on purpose.

He hasn't brought it up. He went straight from the knife on the bed to running bank routing numbers at the kitchen table, and I let him because we needed the information and because I didn't know what to say. We'll talk about it. We always talk about it eventually. But right now we're both in logistics mode and that's where we need to be.

The voicemail came to _his_ phone. The video went to _his_ email. Not mine. She went around me to the person I can't protect the same way I protect myself. That was a choice, and it tells me something about her.

It tells me she understands what he is to me. Which means she's been watching longer than one bad night at a gas station.

[[Raphael]] said the same thing before I did.
