# Camarilla

## Profile

The sect we answer to in Chicago. It runs new Kindred through an education system — which surprised me; I'd expected less structure from people who live this long.

## Court

- **[[Kevin Jackson]]** — Prince of Chicago. He heard our case at the end of the Sterry business: spared [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]], granted us the right of first refusal on a Blood Hunt against [[Walter Nash]], and put us on his radar.
