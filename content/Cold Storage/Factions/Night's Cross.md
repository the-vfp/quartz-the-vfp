# Night's Cross

## Profile

A newish, unaffiliated Chicago crew — not ordinary criminals but vampire hunters. [[Trip]] read the insignia off their armor: a dagger driven through a skull. They work in open violation of the Masquerade. Not government, not the Second Inquisition — but they hold real, working knowledge of how to find and kill our kind.

## Known Members

- The cell [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]] hired for the [[Hercules House]] ambush — plate carriers, balaclavas, suppressed automatic weapons, wooden stakes, a nail bomb. Four killed, one left alive with a broken leg, one fled.

## Notes

We met them in the rafters of [[Hercules House]], the last layer of Paul's trap — camera-guided, disciplined, calling fire to each other. We took the cell apart, but one got out the door, and he knows this coterie exists and that we kill hunters. An organization with anti-Kindred knowledge working in this city is a problem larger than the one night.
