# Anarchs

## Profile

The Kindred who reject Camarilla hierarchy — and in this city a live, organized force, risen sharply since Clan Brujah crossed to their side and the disaffected went looking for someone to follow. Their public face is [[Gengis]], who hides his Kindred work behind crowds of true-believing mortal activists.

## Known Members

- **[[Gengis]]** — figurehead, and an actual leader; loud, brutish, well-funded.
- **[[Collin]]** — Brujah; our own, from the clan now driving the anarch surge, a tension he carries openly.
- **[[House Carna]] / [[Erika]]** — Tremere antitribu, working the edges.
- **Pollock** — a thin-blood I've used as a contact; our background source on Gengis.
- A Brujah elder remembered as **Tyler**, who held this airport in the eighties before leaving for New York; [[Noah Grewal|Fly Boy]] styles himself his heir.

## Notes

The Prince's whole charge to us turns on *not* provoking them — quiet the troublemakers, leave no stains, give no one a reason to call it a rebellion. Our watchman post puts us on the Camarilla side of that line, though I've said aloud that finishing the Prince's task doesn't require the Prince winning totally, every time; the sect loyalties inside this coterie are thin.

What I've seen of their reach: a confrontation already planned around the delegation, a fleet of stolen vehicles staged for it — three trucks and two cars lifted from an impound lot — and crews told to bring bats and follow [[Gengis]]'s cue. And a leader who seems to already know who's coming. Worth keeping: the labyrinth under the city — the old train tunnels more than the sewers — isn't Camarilla ground. The anarchs move down there too.
