# Banu Haqim

## Profile

My clan, and a thin presence in this city. [[Aicha Rana (Sire)|Aicha]] and her sire [[Khadija]] arrived not long before all of this began. Aicha hasn't been quiet about her low opinion of most local Kindred and their moral turpitude — at least among other children of Haqim.

- **[[Aicha Rana (Sire)|Aicha Rana]]** — Khadija's childe; my sire; fixer and guide.
- **[[Claire Evangelista Marte|Myself]]** — Aicha's childe, the youngest of the line here.
