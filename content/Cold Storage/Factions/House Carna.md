# House Carna

## Profile

One of the major Tremere splinter houses, deep in clan lore. It's defined against everything the parent clan is — where Tremere is patriarchal and traditionalist, Carna is feminist, Wiccan, non-traditionalist, and vilified inside the clan for exactly that. Its people sit on both sides of the Camarilla/anarch divide, though it leans anarch, which makes it a quiet bridge more than a clean enemy.

## Known Members

- **[[Erika]]** — antitribu; one of its most important figures, possibly its head.
- **[[Gengis]]** — associated, not necessarily a member; the false Tremere-pawn rumor about him looks like the house's bait.

## Notes

It came to us through [[Alfred Ratimir]]. Its relevance to the job in front of us is sideways: our first plan for [[Gengis]] — leaning on the story that he's a manipulated Tremere puppet — was almost certainly [[Erika]]'s invention, set to make the overconfident overreach. The house itself is a long game: a Tremere faction with a foot in both sects and a habit of winning through information rather than force, which is to say my kind of dangerous.
