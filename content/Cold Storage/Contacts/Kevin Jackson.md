# Kevin Jackson

## Profile

Prince of Chicago, Camarilla. He heard our case the night the Sterry business ended, and he listened closely — most closely to the parts that touched [[Walter Nash]].

## Connections

- **[[Camarilla]]** — he sits at the top of the Chicago court.
- **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]** — spared on our plea, kept as an asset.
- Us — we're on his radar now as people who can handle something delicate and walk out of a hunter ambush we had no business surviving as near-strangers to his court.

## Notes

His first instinct was to have [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]] killed in front of him. [[Trip]]'s last plea, with all of us behind it, turned him. He spared the Sterry family, moved them into an ally's keeping, and put Paul to use against the [[Sabbat]] in Milwaukee.

He rewarded us with his attention and the right of first refusal on a Blood Hunt against [[Walter Nash]].

First time I've stood in front of the Prince. Worth keeping: he wanted a man dead, and a clean plea with the whole coterie behind it moved him off it. He can be moved, if the approach is right.
