# Paul Sterry Sr.

## Profile

For a long time he was only a name at the bottom of the blackmail emails and a calm voice handing out riddles: *Redwood.* Then we stood in his house and he had a face — middle-aged, thick glasses, thinning hair, soft through the middle. Paul Sterry. Father of [[Michelle Sterry]], the fifteen-year-old [[Walter Nash]] fed on and killed. The handle was never anything but a handle.

A mortal who worked out that we're real and turned his grief into a campaign against us. His words, not mine: *"You feel powerless… I can take from you just like you take from me. You took my Michelle."*

## Connections

- **[[Michelle Sterry]]** — his daughter. Her death is the engine under everything he built.
- **[[Walter Nash]]** — killed Michelle. Paul stole Nash's vitae, through a captive ghoul, to run his own operation.
- **[[Jonathan]]** — held him at knifepoint in the hidden office; let him go on [[Trip]]'s plea.
- **[[Night's Cross]]** — the hunters he hired for the ambush at [[Hercules House]].
- **[[Darkhour Security]]** — the surveillance crew he ran as Redwood, out of a building he provided.
- **[[Kevin Jackson]]** — the Prince who spared him and put him to use.

## Notes

A one-man breach of the Masquerade. His dining room was a catalogue: labeled jars of vampire blood and werewolf blood, files on where our loved ones live, ghoul dogs kept from the vein, and one of Nash's own ghouls starving on a padlock in the basement as his vitae supply. He'd been blackmailing other Kindred besides us.

He's also [[Dark Descent]] — the [[verumtenebris.org]] poster whose "werewolf at the harbor" we'd written off as crank noise. He'd been hunting the supernatural for real long before any of us was on his list. Dark Descent was the obsessive's face; Redwood was the operator's.

He resisted [[Trip]]'s Dominate outright — ready for it, hard-minded, and not pretending otherwise: *"Don't try your tricks. I know what you are."* What ended the standoff wasn't a power. It was Trip naming the night's dead and saying *"I'm sorry about what happened to Michelle."*

[[Kevin Jackson]] heard our plea and spared Paul and his family. He sent the wife and son into an ally's keeping and kept Paul himself alive and useful, pointed at the [[Sabbat]] in Milwaukee — the Prince's instrument, never ours. Paul claimed dead-man's-switch drives that dump everything if anyone but him erases them; we watched him delete *something*, and I never confirmed the data was gone. Last I knew, he was still breathing.
