# Jim

## Profile

Gave his name as Jim, then Clarence, then Lance, then back to Jim. He took the pictures of [[Carter]]; he didn't make the calls and didn't know the shape of the operation. I read him as fleeting Melancholic. He kept calling the beating of Carter *a couple of love taps* — and [[Mitch]], reading him, realized the man honestly didn't know "love taps" had meant a hospitalization.

## Connections

- **[[Darkhour Security]]** — hired gig labor, bottom of the chain. Knew [[Redwood]] only as a name.
- **[[Steve Jensen]]** — the one who freelanced the actual beating; Jim was only in the car.

## Notes

He knee'd me in the temple bolting out of the [[Darkhour Office]], then knocked himself cold on the gate. When he woke he talked. I fed from him, just enough to take the edge off the Hunger, and clean against my convictions. He played a panicked call to Redwood's burner — leaned on a middle-school *Pirates of Penzance* run to hold himself together — and the line was already dead.

We let him go. [[Collin]] put a pistol to his temple to make the price of coming back clear; [[Trip]] handed him pills, which he dropped in the first bush past the gate before he vanished. If he turns up dead inside a week, [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]] is tidying loose ends.

[[Trip]] named him again later — *Clarence, with multiple head wounds* — in his tally of the people he'd hurt.
