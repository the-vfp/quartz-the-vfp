# Alfred Ratimir

## Profile

[[Trip]]'s sire, and an old-Chicago Tremere — an immigrant, probably, a photographer for a very long time, with a Godfather-by-way-of-Pacino bearing. Beautifully dressed, a self-rolled cigarette in hand, a habit Trip seems to have caught from him. He keeps an entire floor seventy-odd storeys up and greets guests with *enter freely and of your own free will,* the Dracula line, delivered with a grin that both means it and doesn't. Warm, courtly, and unsettling in the same breath.

## Connections

- **[[Trip]]** — his childe. Alfred wants Trip to succeed and cares little for our actual errands beyond that. Watching them together showed me the Tremere hierarchy plainly: Trip is a different man in his sire's rooms.
- **The coterie** — our Mawla, the mentor we can call on; he receives us at any hour, and helps where it serves Trip.
- **[[Abraham D'Abler]]** — fellow Chicago Tremere; the elder who first set Trip on [[Walter Nash]].

## Notes

He offered us blood drawn live from a deeply compelled servant who opened his own hand to fill the glasses — and handed [[Trip]] vitae rather than mortal blood, which Trip declined. He gave us the true shape of [[Gengis]]: the Tremere-pawn rumor is bait planted by [[Erika]] of [[House Carna]], and the Tremere have no real quarrel with the Banu Haqim here. Then, while Trip took it in, Alfred quietly worked a blood sorcery on him to push his Hunger toward frenzy — a father's power play, toying rather than cruel. The help is real, and so is the reminder of rank; he managed to be generous and dangerous in the same gesture.
