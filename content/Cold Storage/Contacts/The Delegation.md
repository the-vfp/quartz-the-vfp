# The Delegation

## Profile

Two Kindred the Prince has us collecting and guarding — names, clan, and purpose all withheld, which is the point. Everyone who matters is treating them as important without having seen them, and the agreeing-they-matter is the dangerous part. A small-framed younger East Asian woman with long dark hair, and a bald, bearded older white man the Prince called, in writing, "unkempt." They arrive shipped — no one travels as cargo by daylight and survives an unscheduled landing — and walk out through arrivals.

## Connections

- **[[Kevin Jackson|the Prince]]** — sent for them; treats them as worth a coded letter and a watchman crew.
- **[[Noah Grewal|Fly Boy]]** — owed the first call the moment we learn who they are and why they've come.
- **[[Theater Haven]]** — where we hold them through the day; the [[Succubus Club]] is where we hand them over.

## Notes

I don't yet know who they are or what they've come for, and [[Trip]] would rather we never learn — plausible deniability. I'd rather know what's safe to know and curate what gets fed back to Fly Boy. That argument is still open between us.
