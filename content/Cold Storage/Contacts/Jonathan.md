# Jonathan

## Profile

[[Collin]]'s touchstone. Works a transaction counter at a shop tied to Collin — handles customers, knows the regulars. On Collin's surveillance footage he took a call from Collin about online research, opened his laptop, then caught himself: *I'm not this stupid. I have to get off this IP.* He closed it, took it with him, locked up, and walked out under his own power.

## Connections

- **[[Collin]]** — touchstone. Collin hadn't been blackmailed earlier because Jonathan hadn't yet been taken; the night [[Walter Nash]] called in his favors and Collin showed up unsolicited, his office wasn't set up for the first time in their standing arrangement.
- **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]** — the captor. Held Jonathan hooded and tied in the [[Hercules House]] hidden office, a Bowie knife at his throat, until [[Trip]] talked him down.

## Notes

His name was on the blackmail-list sticky note we pulled from the [[Darkhour Office]] — alongside [[Carter]], Raphael, [[Megan]], and [[Gladys Ackerman]]. The list answered the structural question of this whole thing: every one of us was blackmailed because someone we love was on it.

[[Trip]] tracked him by Blood Sorcery — the trail ran through [[Garfield Park]], where he'd been moved, and ended inside [[Hercules House]].

We found him hooded, taped, and tied to a chair in the [[Hercules House]] hidden office, held at knifepoint by [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]. Recovered alive — [[Collin]] kept the hood on him so he'd never see us work, and moved him to safety. The one clean win of the night.
