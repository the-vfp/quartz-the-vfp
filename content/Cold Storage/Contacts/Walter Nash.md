# Walter Nash

## Profile

I've never been in a room with him. Everything I have is secondhand. The first of it was footage out of [[Darkhour Security]] — Nash on camera, attacked by [[Steve Jensen]], Jensen in [[Honored Drinks]] colors. It came to me through the blackmail material, so I hold its framing as hostile: reliable as an image, not as a story. It looked like Nash feeding. That was the wrong verb.

## Connections

- **[[Darkhour Security]]** — produced the footage. Source and interested party both.
- **[[Steve Jensen]]** — the attacker on the tape. Still hasn't woken.
- **[[Honored Drinks]]** — the branding on Jensen. A thread we haven't pulled.
- **[[Spider]]** — sent by a superior to deal with Nash. I don't know whose errand.
- **[[Collin]]** — was on Nash on his own before any of us were put together.

## Notes

The footage wasn't a feeding. It was the killing of [[Michelle Sterry]], fifteen — fed on and left in a park, comatose a month before she died. Her father, [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]], built the blackmail apparatus out of that grief. Nash's vitae fed Paul's dogs; one of Nash's own ghouls was the starved thing in the [[Hercules House]] basement, kept to be handed back to him.

Nash isn't the architect of what was done to us. He's what the architect worked from.

Prince [[Kevin Jackson]] gave us the right of first refusal on a Blood Hunt against him at the close of all this, and we recovered one of his ghouls. Beyond that I have nothing on him I've confirmed with my own senses — no clan, no haven, no face in a room. Setting down what little I have.
