# Dutch

## Profile

[[Trip]]'s ghoul. He was there as an "observer only" when [[Carter]] was beaten — the attackers wanted someone to carry the message back about how serious they were. He brought Carter to [[Trip's Clinic]] afterward.

## Connections

- **[[Trip]]** — his domitor.
- **[[Carter]]** — watched him beaten; got him to care.

## Notes

The night we first met, [[Mitch]] frenzied on the news Dutch carried in — the Beast decided Dutch had been present and hadn't protected Carter, so Dutch was the one it went for. Five nail-blades into his upper chest, neck, and collarbone in a single strike, and a second, worse blow after, before the frenzy broke. He should have died. He didn't. He sat with Carter while the rest of us slept.
