# Felicity Miller

## Profile

The Department of Aviation **Commissioner** — near the top of the chain that can flag, redirect, or turn back an arriving traveler. Mortal. [[Noah Grewal|Fly Boy]]'s lever at O'Hare; named in the Prince's letter as the point of his influence.

## Connections

- **[[Noah Grewal|Fly Boy]]** — claims influence over her, and it's how he reaches into the airport; he has no official authority himself.
- **The delegation** — any order to stop the guests would run across her desk.

## Notes

We met her at her desk past midnight. I sat lower than the others and watched her hands, which is how I caught her finger resting on the security button before she'd decided whether to use it. [[Trip]] compelled her twice, and she walked us up through the authorized-only offices to [[Noah Grewal|Noah]]'s door — *never saw you* — and was gone.

What I came away certain of: she is not blood-bound to him. When he phoned her after we left she was annoyed, dismissive — *stop being so paranoid* — not a thrall's devotion, and the fact that he had to phone at all means there's no ghoul's leash on her either. So whatever Fly Boy actually holds over the Department is leverage or money, the kind of thing that leaves a record, and I don't have it yet. Trip has her face now, if we need another way in.
