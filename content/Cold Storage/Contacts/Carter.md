# Carter

## Profile

[[Mitch]]'s mortal brother. Trapeze performer. Lives in apartment 802 of the eight-story building where the rest of us first met. Full name Carter Marshall.

## Connections

- **[[Mitch]]** — brother. The reason Mitch is in this at all.
- **[[Dutch]]** — carried him into the clinic after the beating.

## Notes

A big blonde man beat him the first night, wanting to know where Mitch sleeps. Carter didn't give it up. Broken nose, hairline fracture in the orbital bone, a fracture in the upper arm above the elbow. He'll live, but the arm puts a trapeze artist out of work for a long time.

He was unconscious at [[Trip's Clinic]] through Mitch's frenzy, spattered with the blood of it and never knowing.

The blackmailers had a full stalker operation on him, run out of the [[Darkhour Office]] — a wall of surveillance photos and printed notes on his movements: *laundry 8–10:30pm, home by 4pm, take liberties red bike.* They knew his week better than he did.

He recovered under [[Mitch]]'s watch. With the blackmail dismantled and the man behind it unmade, the immediate threat to him is over — though [[Trip]] still jokes about billing him for the treatment.
