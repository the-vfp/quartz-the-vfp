# Pollock

## Profile

A thin-blood I keep as a contact on the anarch side. Low in the order and useful precisely for it — close enough to hear things, far enough down that no one minds him hearing. It was his read, layered over what the rest of us could piece together, that let me place [[Gengis]] as a leader and not just a name on a list.

## Connections

- **[[Anarchs]]** — moves among them; my window into them.
- **The coterie** — my contact, not the group's.
