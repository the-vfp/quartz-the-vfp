# Steve Jensen

## Profile

Tall, white, blonde. In the [[Walter Nash]] footage he wore a jacket branded with [[Honored Drinks]]. ID number 633CH.

## Connections

- **[[Darkhour Security]]** — the footage of him attacking Nash was produced there; he was at the [[Darkhour Office]] the night we swept it.
- **[[Walter Nash]]** — attacked him on camera.

## Notes

The big blonde man who beat [[Carter]] the first night matched him — big, blonde, hoodie, tattoos on the neck — but no one made the identification for certain. I'm leaving it open whether "the man who beat Carter" and Steve Jensen are one person or two out of the same outfit.

At the [[Darkhour Office]] he was at the top of the stairs in a gas mask with a gun. [[Spider]] charged him, took three rounds, wrenched the gun aside, tore the mask off, and drained him dry — and broke his own conviction against feeding on the living to do it.

His body rode in [[Trip]]'s trunk afterward, to be called in later as a John Doe. He turned out to be the only [[Darkhour Security]] operative who'd ever met the client face to face. The seven-minute call on his burner was that client; the line went dead the moment Jensen did. The client was [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]. [[Trip]] named Jensen too, among the night's dead.
