# Michelle Sterry

## Profile

Fifteen years old. [[Walter Nash]] fed on her and left her in a park; she lay comatose more than a month before she died. I never knew her. None of us did. By the time her name reached us she was already the reason for everything else.

## Connections

- **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]** — her father. He built the whole operation out of losing her.
- **[[Walter Nash]]** — killed her.

## Notes

Her father set her at the gate as a riddle before he'd let us in: *"how many pints can a 110-pound 15-year-old girl lose before she becomes irrevocably brain-damaged?"* The answer was her. Everything Paul did traces back to a man who lost his daughter to one of us and made the loss into a weapon.
