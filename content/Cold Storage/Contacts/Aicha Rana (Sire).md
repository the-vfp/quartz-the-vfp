# Aicha Rana (Banu Haqim)

My sire. [[Khadija]]'s childe, and as unlike her sire as a childe can be — socially, economically, every way that shows. She carries herself like someone who's only ever known poverty, survival, and small joys, and she is the rarest thing our kind manages: an optimist. New enough to Chicago that she's still learning its shape, and quick about it. She has not done a good job of hiding her low opinion of most local Kindred and their "moral turpitude" — at least not among other children of Haqim.

## Connections

- **[[Khadija]]** — her sire; my grandsire.
- I am her childe.
