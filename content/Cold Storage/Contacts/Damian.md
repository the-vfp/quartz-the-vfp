# Damian

## Profile

The Sheriff of the Chicago Camarilla, and the hand that carries [[Kevin Jackson|the Prince]]'s less comfortable instructions. Usually a laid-back acquaintance we've crossed paths with rarely over the years and more often lately. The night he brought us this job he was the opposite of laid-back — leg tapping, posture too straight, there on orders rather than choice — which I don't think the others caught.

## Connections

- **[[Kevin Jackson|the Prince]]** — serves him directly; compiled the three dossiers that came with the letter.
- **The coterie** — our contact for this job; left the envelope in a fast-food booth and walked.
- **[[Gengis]]** — rumored, alongside the Brujah Primogen Critias, to want him dead.

## Notes

He made the drop — the envelope and four one-use phones — and left without conversation. The nervousness reads as pressure from above: if the Sheriff is on edge handing us an errand, the errand matters more than it looks. First time he's been more than a face to me; I doubt it's the last time we deal with him.
