# Gengis

## Profile

The face of the anarch movement in this city, and — I had this wrong off the file — not one nuisance among three but an actual anarch *leader.* The Prince keeps few of those breathing here on purpose; Gengis is one of the ones who's lasted, well enough known that I'll have his face on sight now. Ugly, pierced, loud, an anti-establishment firebrand who works behind crowds of true-believing mortal activists. His standing has only climbed since Clan Brujah crossed to the anarch side and the disaffected went looking for someone to shout behind. [[Alfred Ratimir]] calls him a brute, and he is, but he's also conspicuously rich, which his kind usually aren't. [[Damian]] and the Brujah Primogen Critias are both said to want him dead.

## Connections

- **[[Anarchs]]** — their figurehead, and more than that: a leader.
- **[[House Carna]] / [[Erika]]** — the rumor that he's a Tremere pawn, mind-seeded against anarch interests, was planted by [[Erika]] to make the arrogant overreach and mishandle her people. The Tremere angle on him is a dead end.
- **[[Collin]]** — same Brujah/anarch fault line; Collin's clan is the one feeding Gengis's rise.
- **[[Kevin Jackson|the Prince]]** — Gengis would gladly loosen his grip; the one most able to turn the delegation pickup into a brawl.

## Notes

Our first idea — steer him through Tremere blood magic — died the moment [[Alfred Ratimir|Alfred]] named the rumor's source. Buying him would mean real money, and his [[Gengis's Villa|Gold Coast villa]] (gated, dogs, Sex Pistols at full volume to spite the block) says he has it. He wasn't home either night.

I found him in person at the [[Mohawk Bar]], up on the mezzanine with a young mortal activist who'd come to brief him on a protest he'd turned up to late. He caught [[Collin]]'s eye across the room and showed him the Beast in his own — *I know what you are* — Kindred, not who; gave Collin a nod, then called him a coward at his back. [[Spider]] got above him and listened: Gengis told his crew to bring bats for tomorrow and to watch him for who to swing on, *a way to make some new friends.* He already knows who's arriving. He's behind the stolen cars staged for the ambush, too. The men we were sent to keep in the dark, he is not keeping in the dark — and I don't yet know who told him.

*(Name turns up variously — Gengis, Genghis, Genjis.)*
