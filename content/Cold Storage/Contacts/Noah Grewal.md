# Noah Grewal

## Profile

Goes by Fly Boy; the given name is barely known. He claims O'Hare and everything that moves through it by air. He isn't Camarilla, the Cam doesn't honor the claim, and until now he hadn't made enough of a nuisance of himself to be worth the trouble. He's said to follow in the footsteps of a Brujah anarch elder called Tyler, who actually held the airport in the eighties before decamping to New York. Fly Boy is the dollar-store version of that.

## Connections

- **[[Felicity Miller]]** — his lever inside the Department of Aviation; anything he wants done at O'Hare runs through her.
- **[[Kevin Jackson|the Prince]]** — named him a troublemaker who might flag or redirect the delegation.
- **The delegation** — arrives through his claimed turf, O'Hare Terminal 5.

## Notes

We didn't go around him in the end; we sat down with him. He met us facing the runway with his back to the room so [[Trip]] couldn't catch his eyes — a small, theatrical man in a white linen suit, claiming a whole airport with no one standing behind him. He knew the guests were being shipped in heavy-duty speaker cases, wanted to learn who was inside them, and dangled locking them up or putting them on a flight to JFK. His opening price was robbery; what he actually wanted was to be *told* — to be inside the thing.

What closed: one of [[Trip]]'s resources (Trip ate the loss) and the first call the moment we learn who the delegation is and why they've come. He asked for the run of our streets to feed for a year, and we refused — we don't get to spend a person a week to make a man feel important. His card is a phone number and nothing else. He watches us through a ghoul on the airport cameras.
