# Charles Dawson

## Profile

"Crook." A Nosferatu of the Camarilla — [[Spider]] had shared a room with him years ago and never caught the name. Hunched, limping, an ugly face wearing a wide, practiced, polite smile. Disarmingly hospitable — *what do you need, something to eat, a place to sleep?* is a line he's said a thousand times and means every time. He trades in favors, and lives by *if Crook smiles, someone's losing money.* He founded the Crooks Lounge chain and keeps his haven under the original branch in Rogers Park. Old, but not ancient.

## Connections

- **The coterie** — we met him scouting the original [[Crooks Lounge]]. *I especially like the word "we" in that sentence,* he told us.
- **[[Kevin Jackson|the Prince]]** — named in the letter as a man to quiet; Crook worked the inverse out on his own — that the guests matter, that we were picked because we're nobodies, and that making their patron miserable would be funny.
- **[[Gengis]]** — other side of the aisle; little crossover, no love, but he won't move against him for free.

## Notes

**The deal:** if the delegation reaches its destination without Crook or his people being seen, each of us owes him a minor boon — five in all. If we're seen, the ledger's clean and he writes nothing down. A bad bargain on its face — a Nosferatu can do enormous harm unseen — but it's how he works: *we don't make deals unless they're weighted heavily in our favor.* He shook on it with [[Trip]], wished us a boring night, and floated future fun (he'd clocked [[Collin]] as a man who likes to blow things up). Mid-pitch he rattled Trip badly enough to set off a fit of paranoia. He stayed out of sight the whole second night. There's a chance he simply leaves us alone — which is the same as a chance.
