# Abraham D'Abler

## Profile

A very old Chicago Tremere, and a rare one — one of the few elders who answered the Beckoning, went east, and came back. Since returning he hasn't rebuilt a chantry or chased a position; he simply does Blood Sorcery for the Camarilla, an old-school loyalist with no appetite for maneuvering. (Americans say "Dabler"; he writes it D'Abler.)

## Connections

- **[[Trip]]** — the elder who first put Trip on the [[Walter Nash]] business, back at the start of all this.
- **[[Camarilla]]** — serves it directly as a blood-sorcerer; the old guard in a single person.
- **[[Banu Haqim]]** — part of why there's essentially no Tremere opposition to my clan in this city.

## Notes

He's the reason Chicago has Tremere power without a true chantry: the Pyramid fell when Vienna burned to the Second Inquisition, leaving only scattered houses — and one returned elder doing the clan's signature work. Not someone we've dealt with directly; I keep him here as the shape of the Tremere ground [[Trip]] walks on.
