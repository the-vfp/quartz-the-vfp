# Officer Williams

## Profile

A city bylaw officer working [[Garfield Park]]. Unremarkable otherwise — just in the wrong place when [[Trip]] needed to feed.

## Connections

- **[[Trip]]** — fed on him.

## Notes

[[Trip]] dominated him under cover of testing "sugar pills," fed on him, set him on a bench, and called *assistance needed* on the man's own radio before walking back to the car. Clean by the rules of the [[Garfield Park]] Elysium — *clean up your mess, but all are free to feed* — but he'll wake in a hospital with a story he can't explain. A loose end.

[[Trip]] counted him by name later, among the people he'd hurt that night. The guilt stuck to him.
