# Dark Descent

## Profile

A handle on [[verumtenebris.org]], not a person — or that's how it read at first. Blurry UFO photos, blurry Loch Ness photos, a post about a "werewolf at the harbor." Almost every image on the board is theirs, mixed in with the mundane — "show me your cat photos" next to the crackpot material.

Raphael ran the forum lead down early and called it a dead end for the time being.

## Notes

The werewolf-at-the-harbor post is the one I can't file under hobbyist paranoia. Everything else could be a lonely crank; that one is harder to dismiss now that I know what's actually out there.

I know now the handle was [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]] — the obsessive's face he wore before he became the operator.
