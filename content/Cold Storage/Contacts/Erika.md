# Erika

## Profile

A Tremere antitribu — a traitor to the clan, in old-Camarilla terms — and one of the most important figures in [[House Carna]], the Tremere splinter that sits firmly on the anarch side. Some say she's its true head. [[Alfred Ratimir|Alfred]] called her frightening, and Alfred is not a man who frightens easily. I've only heard of her secondhand.

## Connections

- **[[House Carna]]** — possibly its leader; feminist, Wiccan, non-traditionalist, reaching across both sides of the Camarilla/anarch line.
- **[[Gengis]]** — associated, not necessarily allied. Alfred's read: the rumor that Gengis is a Tremere pawn turned against anarch interests was Erika's work — bait to make those who think too highly of themselves overreach and mishandle her friends.
- **[[Trip]] / [[Alfred Ratimir]]** — the Tremere thread we pulled, which ran straight into a dead end she'd laid.

## Notes

She wins by shaping what people believe rather than by moving openly, which is the kind of opponent I find hardest to file — there's nothing to catch hold of. We've had no dealings with her and no quarrel yet. But her name is on the board now, attached to a faction with reach into both sects, and I'd rather have the card written before I need it.
