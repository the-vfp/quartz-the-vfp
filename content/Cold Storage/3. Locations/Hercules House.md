# Hercules House

> [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]'s home, and the killbox at the end of "Power Prey." Where [[Jonathan]]'s trail ended.

## Description

A two-story home on a residential cul-de-sac that does **not** match its neighborhood: an eight-foot electrified chain-link fence with **wooden palisade toppers** (deliberate anti-Kindred defense), a flawless paint job on red brick, marble statues of Greek and Roman myth scenes across the yard. A large **Hannah-Barbera Hercules** — the **H** on his belt — stands at the front door like a warning to anyone who'd understand it. Domestic staging that reads as theater: a bicycle on its side, a football by a koi pond, sun-bleached chairs with a magazine, a skateboard with the name **Paul** etched in the deck.

Inside: burgundy walls, Persian rugs, a skylight, a gas-candle chandelier, a grandfather clock at the end of the hall — and clean rectangles on the walls where every family photo had been taken down. The owner stripped his own home of identifying faces because he knew the coterie was coming.

## The trap (Session 4)

The whole house was built to kill this coterie specifically:

- **Ghoul dogs** — two oversized German shepherds (ghouled on stolen vitae) in the front yard. Killed in the opening fight.
- **Tripwire + grandfather clock** — sprang a cheesecloth charge of cornflour into a fireball down the front hall.
- **Stair-mounted stake-rake** — a spring-loaded garden rake fitted with wooden stakes, set to swing down on whoever climbed. [[Mitch]] caught it on his arms and kept it.
- **The [[Knights Cross]] ambush** — six suppressed shooters in the rafters, camera-guided, who rappelled down with stakes. Four killed, one fled, one left alive.
- **Hidden cameras** throughout, feeding targeting to the operator.

## Layout

Ground floor: trapped entry hall, a den/dining room to the left, a kitchen to the right, a straight staircase to an open second-floor mezzanine with many doors. Behind the master-bedroom wall — accessed by a disguised panel in the corner by a wardrobe — a small **hidden office** where [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]] held [[Jonathan]] hostage. A **dining room** cache of catalogued vampire and werewolf blood. A **basement** holding [[Walter Nash]]'s starved, padlocked ghoul.

## Notes

The "female voice" overheard arguing from the gate in [[Session 3]] was most likely one of the [[Knights Cross]] hunters staged inside (at least one of the cell was female) — not, as first assumed, a member of Paul's family. Resolved in [[Session 4]]: the dogs and hunters cleared, [[Jonathan]] recovered, Paul taken alive, the house's blood cache and surveillance seized. The coterie made a great deal of noise doing it — a Masquerade liability they left behind.
