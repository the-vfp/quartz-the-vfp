# Collin's Shop

> [[Collin]]'s gunsmithing space. Behind a roll-up door in the gang district. The coterie's first quiet hour in [[Session 3]].

## Description

A gunsmithing workshop behind a roll-up door in the gang district, with a workbench, a back room, and its own surveillance feed. [[Jonathan]] works the transaction-counter side of [[Collin]]'s operation.

## Notes

In [[Session 3]] the coterie regrouped here. [[Trip]] ran his tracking-ritual prep on the workbench (bloody gauze, scalpel, vial); [[Collin]] went into the back to scrub his surveillance footage — which showed [[Jonathan]] walking out under his own power after catching himself online. [[Spider]], [[Trip]], and [[Mitch]] had their quiet exchange in the bay, three monsters recognizing each other.

It's here that [[Collin]] took the kidnapper's *tick tock* call and gave in to a fury frenzy — destroying his phone before [[Spider]] and [[Mitch]] grappled him and [[Claire]] talked him down.
