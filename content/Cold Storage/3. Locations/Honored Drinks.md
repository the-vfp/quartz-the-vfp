# Honored Drinks

> Bar. Branded on the clothing worn by [[Steve Jensen]] in the [[Walter Nash]] footage.

## Description

Unvisited. Known only through the back of a jacket on camera.

## Notes

Was one of three open leads at the top of [[Session 2]] — Honored Drinks, the [[Darkhour Office]], and [[verumtenebris.org]]. The coterie chose Darkhour. Honored Drinks remains untouched.

[[Steve Jensen]], the man whose jacket carried the branding, died in [[Session 2]]. The bar itself was never visited across the *Power Prey* arc and stays an open thread into Story 2.

Also appears as "Honoured Drink" — a spelling/singular variant.
