# Carter's Apartment

> Eight-story building. Apartment 802. [[Carter]]'s home and the coterie's first meeting place.

## Description

The coterie gathered here in [[Session 1]] to compare notes and watch surveillance footage. [[Collin]] found a planted camera in the unit and recovered months of footage — a woman setting it up, crawling out of a crawlspace, going back in; a tall white man with [[Honored Drinks]] branding on his jacket attacking [[Carter]]; [[Walter Nash]] on camera feeding.

After reviewing the footage, [[Collin]] set up the recovered camera again as a trap. [[Dutch]] stayed to watch over Carter while the coterie slept.

## Notes

The woman from the footage ("crawlspace lady") came out of a wall during daylight, so she's mortal. She doesn't seem to have known what she did.

Ground has been covered and moved off from. If surveillance continues, it's remotely via the camera.
