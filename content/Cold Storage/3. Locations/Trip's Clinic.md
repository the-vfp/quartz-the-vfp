# Trip's Clinic

> Private medical clinic with [[Trip]]'s apartment above it. Site of the [[Session 1]] masquerade breach.

## Description

Runs a real medical practice — enough of a going concern that it has code protocols for restraining violent patients and a herd of gang-affiliated clientele. Staff include [[Antonio]], [[Lorena]], and [[Victor]].

## Notes

In [[Session 1]], [[Mitch]] frenzied in the clinic and tore into [[Dutch]]. Trip called a code, cut his own palm for Scorpion's Touch, and drove a scalpel into Mitch — ineffective against another Kindred. He fell into a hunger compulsion mid-combat, tried to spit venom, and eventually the frenzy broke on a clutch willpower check.

Aftermath: Trip drained a gang patient in the back room in front of arriving security staff — a messy critical — then used Compel and hush money on the ~10 witnesses. Staff were drugged or paid off. "A problem deferred, not solved."

[[Carter]] was recovering here in [[Session 2]] with [[Mitch]] watching over him.

One dot of [[Trip]]'s Herd was temporarily lost from the emergency feeding.

[[Mitch]] later moved [[Carter]] out to a safehouse — partly to keep him away from [[Trip]] after the frenzy. The deferred breach from [[Session 1]] (drugged witnesses, paid-off staff, Compel-managed security) remains unresolved business hanging over the clinic.
