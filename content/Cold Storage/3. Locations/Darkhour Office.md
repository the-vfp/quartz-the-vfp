# Darkhour Office

> Two-story building in Ravenswood. [[Darkhour Security]]'s on-paper address. Not run as an actual business.

## Description

Desolate block — flickering lampposts, abandoned crane jobs, blinking blue security lights above nothing. Fifteen-to-eighteen-foot solid steel fence, locked gate, no employee lot, blinds drawn. A second-floor light shut off as the coterie rounded the back.

Inside: no visible cameras, no discrete cameras, no alarm panel. A window latch that wasn't even locked. Recently-moved-into, mid-renovation, maybe a couple of weeks lived in — though the company on paper has existed longer.

Front room: a CRT monitor rigged to a PC tower, a closed laptop, a jacket matching [[Steve Jensen]]'s from the Nash footage, and a wall covered in surveillance photos of [[Carter]] with sticky notes — *laundry 8–10:30pm*, *home by 4pm*, *take liberties red bike*. A printed email used as a paperweight under the CRT.

## Notes

Session 2's firefight happened on these stairs and in this yard. Tear gas from the landing, gunfire both directions, the back yard where [[Claire Evangelista Marte|Claire]] chased the runner. The PC tower and the printed papers are still sitting there — the coterie left before they could search.

Three silhouettes in a parked car watched the building from across the street on approach. They never engaged and their identity is unknown.

End of session left [[Spider]] over [[Steve Jensen]]'s body and two live captives at the coterie's feet.

[[Session 3]] resolved here before the coterie moved on: they interrogated the captured [[Jim]], pried up a hidden sticky note under the oldest [[Carter]] photo — **the blackmail list** ([[Carter]], [[Raphael]], [[Megan]], [[Gladys Ackerman]], [[Jonathan]]) — and confirmed the building itself had been *bought for [[Darkhour Security]] by the client*, [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]. With Jensen dead and the burner line cut, Darkhour was a dead end; the coterie stripped the office of photos, notes, emails, and the laptop and left.
