# Garfield Park

> The Conservatory is an Elysium. The trail to [[Jonathan]] ran straight through it.

## Description

A public park on Chicago's West Side. The **Conservatory** is designated Elysium — Camarilla neutral ground. Both [[Trip]] and [[Spider]] recognized the status on arrival: *clean up your mess, but all are free to feed.*

## Notes

In [[Session 3]], [[Trip]]'s tracking ritual led the coterie here; [[Jonathan]]'s trail came in to a bench and left a different way — he'd been moved through, not held here.

[[Trip]] fed on **Officer Williams** in the park under the cover of testing pills. [[Claire]] had argued against feeding on an unhoused person *before* the Elysium status was confirmed, and did not retract the argument once it was — she watched without a word and kept her Heightened Senses up.

**Mechanical note:** while in the park, all rolls to resist hunger or frenzy gain +1 die (Elysium grace).
