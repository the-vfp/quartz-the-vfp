# Session 4 — Power Prey (Conclusion)

## Session Summary

Tyler opened on a thesis. *Every vampire tells themselves the same lie — not the big one, not "I'm still human," but something smaller. "There's a line I won't cross."* And the line moves, he said, the moment someone takes something from you. *Tonight you're finally going to get answers. The door is right there. The question is no longer whether you'll get what you came for. It's what part of yourselves you'll leave behind when you do.*

The gate stood open and the two ghoul dogs sat on the lawn, called off. [[Collin]] had his pistol out before anyone agreed on a plan. [[Trip]] put a hand on it — *"This is a family that's hurting"* — and Collin didn't budge: *"You do you. I'll do me. My friend is in there. I'm going in."* Trip: *"I understand."* Collin: *"No, you don't."* He holstered the gun and drew a knife instead. [[Spider]] went up the brick to read the second-floor windows and came away with nothing — the glass had been deliberately blocked. They settled a marching order: [[Mitch]] first, hands buried in his jacket pockets to hide the talons, [[Claire]] behind him *because this man is a father and I'm a mother*, Trip third, Spider on the wall, Collin hanging back at the gate.

Hanging back was the mistake. The instant Mitch's foot hit the porch step and the dogs registered that the group had split, a command sounded, the front door slammed, and both shepherds lunged — one for Claire's throat, one for Trip. Claire blurred sideways on Celerity and still felt teeth find her neck; a chunk of it opened up *much like Steve Jensen on a warehouse floor*. Two aggravated, throat torn, Impaired for the rest of the night and unhealable without blood she refused to take. Trip drew his scalpel down his own palm and stabbed poison into the second dog's shoulder. Then the coterie fell on them. Mitch's claws came out of his pockets. Collin came in on Celerity swinging [[Jim]]'s confiscated baton. And [[Spider]] — *named for Arachne but in that moment something closer to a great ape* — brought both hands down on the join of neck and shoulder with Potence behind them and severed a dog's head with a single sickening pop. The second went down a round later under everyone at once. To a neighbor glancing out a window: two dogs asleep on the lawn.

It was the dogs that told them who they were dealing with. A ghoul is made by feeding a creature vampire blood, regularly, from the vein — not poured from a jar, not done quickly. Whoever lived here had a standing supply of Kindred vitae. *The easiest answer is this guy is also Kindred. He lost someone immortal, the same way he's threatening to take ours.*

The house was a trap built for them specifically. Burgundy walls, Persian rugs, a grandfather clock ticking at the end of the hall, a skateboard by the door with the name **Paul** etched into the deck. And on the walls, with her senses up, Claire saw the clean rectangles where pictures had hung before someone took them down — a home stripped of every identifying face. They hadn't been removed in grief. They'd been removed because the owner knew the coterie was coming and wouldn't leave images of his people lying around. *You are a mouse deciding how you want to walk into the trap.* The traps were literal: a tripwire Mitch's Gangrel instincts caught a half-second late, springing the clock to fire a cheesecloth charge of cornflour into a fireball that roared down the hall past every face (everyone held against the fear frenzy, Mitch on willpower); and a spring-loaded garden rake fitted with wooden stakes, mounted to swing down on whoever climbed the stairs. Mitch took the stairs anyway, bounding, arms up, and caught the rake against his forearms — a stake through the meat but not the heart. He snapped a length off and kept it.

Then the rafters opened up. Six shooters with suppressed automatic weapons, fed targeting by hidden cameras, on the command *"Concentrate fire."* Spider caught a scope-glint mid-leap — *that's not the skylight, that's a rifle* — and took rounds across his body. A second voice called *"Fire in the hole"* and dropped a nail bomb to the ground floor beside Claire. The shooters rappelled down on ropes, drew stakes, and closed: plate carriers, black balaclavas, no agency markings — just a patch on every shoulder, **a dagger driven through a skull**.

What followed was a brawl up and down a staircase. Collin ran the railing on Celerity, both handguns out. Spider, still unseen, grabbed a shooter's rifle and walked his aim into his own teammate before the move broke his Obfuscate and made him visible. Mitch hooked a hunter with the stake-rake and shoved him into the man Collin was fighting; the second went over the broken railing and landed headfirst, neck snapped — *"That was your fault."* The kill came on a messy critical, and the Beast took its cut: a Harm Compulsion settled over Mitch, the certainty that none of these people get to sleep it off, that every one of them dies here tonight. Spider heaved a hunter bodily off the balcony onto one of the two men cornering Claire. Trip kept stabbing and kept rolling poison that kept not taking — he dropped his men with the blade and the rail instead, refusing to kill: *"I want to take him down, but I don't want to fucking kill this man."* One hunter got Claire by the collar. She didn't fight his grip; she leaned into the worst thing in the room — Spider, freed up and turning toward him — and told him to run. He looked at his dead, said *"fuck it,"* and bolted out the door.

That left Mitch's compulsion. He went for the last breathing hunter, the unconscious one Trip had just spared, baring his teeth — *"Get out of my way."* Trip stood in the body's way, let Mitch push past, and drove the poison scalpel into Mitch's neck to try to break the spell. The poison failed one more time. Mitch killed the man, came down off it at zero hunger, and carried out of the room a **Stain on his Humanity** for a kill the compulsion made and his convictions wouldn't forgive.

When it was over: four hunters dead, one fled, one alive at the bottom of the pile with a leg bent the wrong way. Trip read the skull-and-dagger off the armor — the **Knights Cross**, an up-and-coming gang of *vampire hunters*. Not government, not Second Inquisition, not professionals exactly — but people with real, factual knowledge of how to kill Kindred, working in open violation of the Masquerade. Collin clocked the cameras and called them the priority.

Claire took the house apart by ear. Behind a wall in the master bedroom: a muffled, gagged voice and a second person breathing in tight control. Behind another door: two frightened people, holding it together. One more muffled voice down in the basement. The master bedroom itself was a fake-out — empty, expensive, the blood-trail dead-ending at a blank wall — until Claire found the seam of a hidden panel disguised in the corner and the side of a wardrobe (Collin remembering the wall-crawler from [[Carter's Apartment]]). Behind it, a small office stinking of sweat: **[[Jonathan]]** tied to a chair, hooded and taped, and behind him a middle-aged man, thick glasses, thinning hair, a Bowie knife at Jonathan's throat.

*"How many pints do you think my daughter lost?"*

Claire reached for Dominate and hit a wall — the man was ready for it, his mind hard, and as the blade drew a bead of blood from Jonathan's neck he snarled, *"Don't try your tricks. I know what you are."* He wanted them to understand what powerlessness felt like. *"You took my Michelle."* And then Trip talked. Not a roll's worth of charm — a confession. He named the wreckage of the last two nights out loud — [[Officer Williams]] on a bench, [[Jim|Clarence]] with his head split, the man Collin shot, [[Steve Jensen]] who isn't waking up — and said the thing nobody else would: *"I don't remember the people I've helped half as well as the people I've hurt. I just want this to be done. I'm sorry about what happened to Michelle. I am."* The knife came down. The man dropped it, backed into his desk, and sat.

His name is **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]** — *Redwood* was only ever a handle. His fifteen-year-old daughter **[[Michelle Sterry|Michelle]]** is the girl [[Walter Nash]] fed on and dumped in a park; she lay comatose more than a month before she died. A grieving mortal who had figured out vampires were real and built an entire operation to make them hurt: blackmail leverage, hired hunters, ghoul dogs maintained on stolen vitae — the blood coming from one of Nash's own people, a starved ghoul padlocked into a latex suit in the basement, mouth sewn shut, kept hungry. He was a one-man Masquerade breach with a dining room full of catalogued, labeled jars of vampire and *werewolf* blood, and files on where other Kindred's loved ones lived. He had blackmailed more vampires than just this coterie.

What to do with him was the hardest call of the night. Claire wanted him dead — he knew what they were, he knew where her family was, he'd armed men to stake them — but she balked at the cold logic of leveraging his surviving wife and son for political credit. The coterie agreed not to make the call alone. They'd research him first, keep their own copy of everything, keep him *theirs* — and then hand him up the chain. The Prince of Chicago, **[[Kevin Jackson]]**, heard the case with real interest, particularly the parts about Nash. His instinct was to have Paul killed in front of him. One last plea from Trip — the whole coterie standing behind it — turned him. Jackson spared the Sterry family, sent them away into an ally's keeping, and kept Paul *"Mr. Redwood" Sterry* alive and useful: he'll be pointed at the **Sabbat in Milwaukee**. And he rewarded the coterie for handling something delicate and surviving a hunter ambush as near-strangers to his court — they now have his attention, and the **right of first refusal on a Blood Hunt against Walter Nash.**

*"We'll wrap our story Power Prey there."* Jonathan is home. The blackmailer is unmade and repurposed. The family lives. And the coterie that was forced together by a dead girl's grieving father walks into the next chapter with the Prince watching and Nash's name already circled.
