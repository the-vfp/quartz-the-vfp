# Carter's Apartment

## Description

Eight-story building, apartment 802 — [[Carter]]'s place, and where the five of us first sat down together to compare what we had and watch the footage. [[Collin]] found a planted camera in the unit and pulled months off it: a woman setting the equipment up, crawling out of a crawlspace and back into it; a tall white man with [[Honored Drinks]] on his jacket beating [[Carter]]; and what we took at the time for [[Walter Nash]] feeding.

Once we'd seen it, Collin set the camera back up as a trap of our own. [[Dutch]] stayed to watch over Carter while we slept.

## Notes

The woman from the footage — the crawlspace lady — came out of the wall in daylight, so she's mortal, and from the look of her she didn't know what she was doing. Someone runs her.

We've covered this ground and moved off it. If anyone's still watching the place, it's through that camera.
