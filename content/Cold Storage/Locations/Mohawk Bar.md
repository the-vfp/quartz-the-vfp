# Mohawk Bar

## Description

A grimy British-invasion punk dive frozen in whatever year it peaked — graffiti, old band posters, a 2.3-star reputation and an open dislike of newcomers. Not gang-tied, and not a known Kindred haunt; just a bar with a temper. A matchbox out of [[Gengis's Villa]] led us here.

## Notes

[[Gengis]] holds court on the mezzanine. We went in carefully and it still went sideways — [[Collin]] read as a cop the second he opened his mouth and the conversations died around him; [[Spider]] got a beer tipped down his hood and a sneer for his trouble. So Spider turned the bust into the job: into the dark, up under the perforated-metal walkway on his grip, and he hung there and listened. What he brought down is the night's real haul — Gengis already knows who arrives tomorrow, told his crew to bring bats and to watch him for who to swing on, and someone came up the stairs to say *the cars will be ready.* It's his staging ground as much as his bar.
