# Crooks Lounge

## Description

A bar in Rogers Park on the north side, shut and barred, a *wear safety equipment* sign on the door. It's the original location of the Crooks Lounge chain — the name is not a coincidence — founded by the man who calls himself [[Charles Dawson|Crook]]. (The Crooks Lounge where we once met [[Walter Nash]] is a different branch of the same franchise.)

Sealed against vermin the way bars are, except for a high rear vent and a back door left merely locked. Below it, a damp brick basement that smells of rot and old blood — Crook's haven, simple and stereotypically Nosferatu, which tells me he isn't an especially old one. There's at least one hidden way in we couldn't find.

## Notes

We scouted it through [[Trip]]'s spy-rat, which caught Crook feeding on a young blonde man in the basement — a feeding that left no trace at all by the time he let us in minutes later. [[Spider]] was made at the back door and the meeting turned cordial, and it's where we struck the favor deal: five boons owed if the delegation reaches its end unseen by him. As a piece of Nosferatu discipline it's worth studying against our own [[Theater Haven|haven]].
