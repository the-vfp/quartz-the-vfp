# Darkhour Office

## Description

Two-story building in Ravenswood, [[Darkhour Security]]'s address on paper — not a working business. A desolate block: flickering lampposts, abandoned crane jobs, blue security lights blinking over nothing. A fifteen-to-eighteen-foot solid steel fence, locked gate, no employee lot, blinds down. A second-floor light went out as we came around the back.

Inside: no visible cameras, no hidden ones, no alarm panel, a window latch left unlocked. Recently moved into, mid-renovation — a couple of weeks lived in, though the company's been on paper longer.

Front room: a CRT wired to a tower, a closed laptop, a jacket matching [[Steve Jensen]]'s from the Nash footage, and a wall of surveillance photos of [[Carter]] with sticky notes — *laundry 8–10:30pm, home by 4pm, take liberties red bike.* A printed email holding down the desk under the CRT.

## Notes

The firefight happened on these stairs and in this yard — gas from the landing, gunfire both ways, the back lot where I ran down the fleeing man. We left before we could search; the tower and the papers were still sitting there.

Three silhouettes watched the building from a parked car across the street as we came in. They never moved, and I don't know who they were.

We came back to finish it: questioned [[Jim]], pried up a hidden sticky note under the oldest [[Carter]] photo — the blackmail list ([[Carter]], Raphael, [[Megan]], [[Gladys Ackerman]], [[Jonathan]]) — and confirmed the building had been bought for [[Darkhour Security]] by the client, [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]. With Jensen dead and the burner cut, Darkhour was a dead end. We stripped it of photos, notes, emails, and the laptop, and left.
