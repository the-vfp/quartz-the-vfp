# Gengis's Villa

## Description

A three-storey villa on the **Gold Coast**, behind the gate of an upscale community — several homes, a manned intercom, ring cams, a pole camera on the cul-de-sac; ordinary affluent security, not a fortress. [[Gengis]]'s is the obvious house: the Sex Pistols at full volume to spite the neighbors, a gravel drive, cars inside the gate, guard dogs on the grounds. A conspicuously bourgeois address for an anarch firebrand. The dossier gives it as **126 E. Cedar Street**; [[Alfred Ratimir]] misremembered it as 125.

## Notes

We went the first night; Gengis wasn't home. Up-close cameras made surveillance risky, so [[Collin]] set standoff cameras from a distance. [[Spider]] scaled in with Celerity and Potence and slipped his rat through a cracked window; he found nothing useful, and with his Hunger climbing he put a fist through the marble kitchen island hard enough to spring a hidden compartment — a single matchbox stamped [[Mohawk Bar]] inside, the night's only parting thread.

The garage told its own story: two Harley-Davidsons, a blacked-out Lexus, an old orange-yellow Honda — and no empty stall. Wherever Gengis went that night, he didn't drive himself.
