# Trip's Clinic

## Description

A private medical clinic with [[Trip]]'s apartment above it — a real practice, enough of one to have protocols for restraining violent patients and a herd of gang-affiliated regulars. Staff include [[Antonio]], [[Lorena]], and [[Victor]].

## Notes

[[Mitch]] frenzied here the first night and tore into [[Dutch]]. Trip called a code, opened his own palm for Scorpion's Touch, and drove a scalpel into Mitch — useless against another of us. He slipped into a hunger compulsion mid-fight, tried to spit venom, and the frenzy finally broke.

After: Trip drained a gang patient in the back room in front of his own arriving staff, then leaned on Compel and hush money to manage the ten or so witnesses. Some were drugged, some paid. He called it a problem deferred, not solved. The emergency feeding cost him part of his herd.

[[Carter]] recovered here afterward, [[Mitch]] watching over him. The breach itself — drugged witnesses, paid-off staff, security smoothed over with Compel — Trip deferred rather than solved.
