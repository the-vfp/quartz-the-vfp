# Hercules House

## Description

A two-story home on a residential cul-de-sac that does not match its street: an eight-foot electrified chain-link fence topped with wooden palisades — a deliberate measure against our kind — flawless paint on red brick, marble statues of Greek and Roman myth across the yard. A big Hanna-Barbera Hercules, the H on his belt, stands at the door like a warning to anyone who'd read it. The domestic staging is theater: a bicycle on its side, a football by a koi pond, sun-bleached chairs and a magazine, a skateboard with the name Paul cut into the deck.

Inside: burgundy walls, Persian rugs, a skylight, a gas-candle chandelier, a grandfather clock at the end of the hall — and clean rectangles on the walls where every family photo had hung. He stripped his own home of faces because he knew we were coming.

## The trap

The whole house was built to kill this coterie in particular:

- **Ghoul dogs** — two oversized German shepherds, ghouled on stolen vitae, in the front yard. Killed in the opening fight.
- **Tripwire and grandfather clock** — sprang a cheesecloth charge of cornflour into a fireball down the front hall.
- **Stair-mounted stake-rake** — a spring-loaded garden rake fitted with wooden stakes, set to drop on whoever climbed. [[Mitch]] caught it on his arms and kept it.
- **The [[Night's Cross]] ambush** — six suppressed shooters in the rafters, camera-guided, rappelling down with stakes. Four dead, one fled, one left alive.
- **Hidden cameras** throughout, feeding the operator targeting.

## Layout

Ground floor: the trapped entry hall, a den/dining room to the left, a kitchen to the right, a straight staircase to an open second-floor mezzanine lined with doors. Behind the master-bedroom wall, through a disguised panel by a wardrobe, a small hidden office where [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]] held [[Jonathan]]. A dining room cache of catalogued vampire and werewolf blood. A basement holding [[Walter Nash]]'s starved, padlocked ghoul.

## Notes

The female voice we heard arguing from the gate I first took for someone of Paul's family. The house turned out to hold only Paul and the [[Night's Cross]] cell, so it was likely one of them. By the end the dogs and hunters were down, [[Jonathan]] was out, Paul was taken alive, and the blood cache and cameras were ours. We made a great deal of noise doing it, and left a Masquerade problem behind us.
