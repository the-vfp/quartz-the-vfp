# Theater Haven

## Description

A neglected theater [[Spider]] and [[Mitch]] live in, posted as *under renovation, new ownership* with the front boarded against vagrants and curiosity — a handsome facade and a strip of gone-to-seed green out front. Spider put the most into it; [[Collin]] built the security; a small crew of Nosferatu hands did the rest. Spider roosts in the attic over the house; Mitch keeps a small room. [[Trip]], Collin, and I keep our own havens elsewhere. There's a replanted garden, an unobtrusive postern that empties near a manhole for straight-down sewer access, and a real alarm system.

## Notes

This is where we've decided to hold the delegation through the day before we deliver them — the safest place from Crook once they're inside our ground. The risks I'm watching: [[Gengis]] hitting us before we ever reach the theater, and the worse one, leading an anarch warband to a haven the Prince granted us. [[Dutch]] is dressing it for guests — two rooms for two day-sleepers, films, the rest. I'm the one staying with them.
