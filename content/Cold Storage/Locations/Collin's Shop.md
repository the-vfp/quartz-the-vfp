# Collin's Shop

## Description

[[Collin]]'s gunsmithing space, behind a roll-up door in the gang district — workbench, a back room, its own camera feed. [[Jonathan]] works the counter side of his operation.

## Notes

We regrouped here for our first quiet hour. [[Trip]] laid out his tracking work on the bench — bloody gauze, scalpel, a vial; [[Collin]] went into the back to scrub his own footage, which showed [[Jonathan]] walking out under his own power after he caught himself online. [[Spider]], Trip, and [[Mitch]] had their low exchange in the bay — three monsters recognizing each other.

It's also where [[Collin]] took the kidnapper's *tick tock* call and went into a frenzy, smashing his phone before Spider and Mitch pinned him and I talked him down.
