# Garfield Park

## Description

A public park on the West Side. The Conservatory is Elysium — Camarilla neutral ground. Both [[Trip]] and [[Spider]] knew it on sight: *clean up your mess, but all are free to feed.*

## Notes

[[Trip]]'s tracking work led us here. [[Jonathan]]'s trail came in to a bench and left another way — he'd been moved through, not held.

[[Trip]] fed on [[Officer Williams]] in the park, under cover of testing pills. I'd argued against feeding on an unhoused man before the Elysium status was confirmed, and I didn't take the argument back once it was. I watched without a word and kept my senses up.
