# Mitch

## Profile

Gangrel, and [[Carter]]'s brother. The Beast shows on him when it rises — ears and arms lengthening, hands going to dagger-claws. His gifts run to those claws and to Fortitude.

## Connections

- **[[Carter]]** — his mortal brother, the trapeze artist. The leverage the rest of us were threatened with.
- **[[Trip]]** and **[[Spider]]** — the two who keep pulling him back from the edge.
- **The coterie** — [[Claire Evangelista Marte|me]], [[Trip]], [[Spider]], [[Collin]].

## Notes

He frenzied the first night, on the news of [[Carter]]'s beating, and tore into [[Dutch]] at [[Trip's Clinic]] — came back to himself in a wrecked clinic full of witnesses, with [[Trip]]'s silence for company. It marked his Humanity, though he held the line against losing a point of it.

At the [[Darkhour Office]] he arrived on foot, nearly run down by the fleeing Charger; he read the captured [[Jim]] well enough to put his scalpel away once he saw the man truly hadn't grasped what had been done to Carter; he helped [[Spider]] wrestle [[Collin]] out of a frenzy; and at the [[Hercules House]] gate he caught what the rest of us missed — *those aren't dogs* — naming the shepherds as ghouls before [[Trip]] could step through.

At the house he caught a spring-loaded stake-rake on his forearms, heart unpierced, and kept it. Then the Beast took a kill out of him — the certainty that every hunter there had to die that night — and it cost him another stain on his Humanity. He's struggling with it openly now: every frenzy leaves a permanent mark on his body, rare even for a Gangrel, and he's started asking for help out loud. *I owe you*, he told Trip.
