# Collin

## Profile

Brujah, and the one of us who was never blackmailed. He was working [[Walter Nash]] on his own and walked into the rest of us sideways. He talks through handwritten notes at his shop. He knows his way around a gun — we all clocked, watching him in the office, that it wasn't his first firefight. His people went at [[Darkhour Security]] early and bounced off their online security.

## Connections

- **[[Jonathan]]** — his touchstone; a brother in all but blood, the two of them raised together. Taken to force Collin into our situation, and recovered.
- **The coterie** — [[Claire Evangelista Marte|me]], [[Trip]], [[Mitch]], [[Spider]].

## Notes

At the [[Darkhour Office]] he dropped the lead man through the front door with a called shot, then put one at the legs of the runner — who juked wrong and knocked himself out on the gate.

At his shop he scrubbed his own footage and found [[Jonathan]] had walked out under his own power — *I'm not this stupid* — before he was taken. Then the kidnapper's distorted *tick tock* call came, and he went into a frenzy, smashing the phone before [[Spider]] and [[Mitch]] pinned him and I got in his face and refused to let him fall.

At [[Hercules House]] he wouldn't wait at the gate — *my friend is in there* — ran the railing with a gun in each hand, and when a hunter asked what we were, told him: *just vampire hunters.* He brought [[Jonathan]] out of the hidden office alive. The one clean win of the night.
