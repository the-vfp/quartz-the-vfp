# Claire Evangelista Marte

## Profile

Spare and direct. I go still and very focused when a situation needs action rather than drama. [[Aicha Rana (Sire)|Aicha]] embraced me about ten years ago. I read my Blood Sorcery the way I read any other source — blood as data to be parsed, not mysticism to be performed; a librarian who learned to read a different kind of record. When I hunt, I pursue: I choose someone and I stay with them.

## Connections

- **Raphael** — my husband. He knows what I am. My Touchstone and my partner in every sense; we think in tandem, and I keep nothing from him.
- **Holly** and **Joy** — my daughters, nineteen and seventeen. They do *not* know.
- **[[Aicha Rana (Sire)|Aicha Rana]]** — my sire.
- **The coterie** — [[Trip]], [[Mitch]], [[Spider]], [[Collin]]. We were forced together under shared blackmail.

## Notes

What I did across the Sterry business, for the record.

I spotted the watchers in the parked car outside the [[Darkhour Office]] on our approach, and when the office turned into a firefight I chased the runner out the back — lost him, took his knee to my temple for it — but caught the Charger driver's face as she fled.

I read the captured [[Jim]]'s blood and fed off him lightly, clean against my convictions. When [[Collin]] went into a frenzy at his shop, I put my face in his and brought him back with leadership, not gentleness. I held the line on feeding ethics in [[Garfield Park]] until I knew the ground was Elysium.

At [[Hercules House]] I took a ghoul dog's teeth in my throat and couldn't close the wound without blood I wouldn't take; the gunfire left my hearing wrecked for the night, and I found the hidden office by ear anyway. I talked one of the hunters into running rather than dying. When it was over I argued against handing Paul's surviving wife and son to the Prince for standing — and I fed from a living hunter, setting aside my own rule against drinking from the vein.
