# Spider

## Profile

Nosferatu. A superior sent him to look into [[Walter Nash]]. He has some history with [[Darkhour Security]] — they were looking for *him* at one point — that he's never fully explained, and that may or may not tie into all this. Early on he'd wanted to ask [[Trip]] about an easier way to feed; he wasn't eating well. After the clinic went the way it did, none of us raised it again.

## Connections

- **The coterie** — [[Claire Evangelista Marte|me]], [[Trip]], [[Mitch]], [[Collin]].

## Notes

At the [[Darkhour Office]] he went straight up the stairs into the gunfire, took three rounds to the gut, tore the gas mask off the man at the top, and drained him dry. It was [[Steve Jensen]], and it was Spider's first kill. He broke his own rule against feeding on the living to get there, and the night left him on all fours over the body, asking [[Trip]] whether he'd ruined everything.

He sat with it and held himself together. After, he climbed the [[Hercules House]] brick like the name promises, helped pull [[Collin]] out of a frenzy, and told [[Mitch]] — another monster having a bad night — *I got your back*, and meant it.

At the house he became our heavy: he took a ghoul dog's head off with a single blow, walked a hunter's own rifle around into the man's ally, and threw another hunter off the balcony. He ended the night spent and riding a hunger he couldn't put down.
