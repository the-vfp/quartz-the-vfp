# Trip

## Profile

Tremere. Runs a private medical clinic with rooms above it. Pragmatic, with a reflex for damage control. He showed the rest of us what he was without quite meaning to — a ritual with a glass vial and a single spider on our way into the Darkhour office, and none of us bothered to look surprised. His Blood Sorcery runs to Scorpion's Touch — poison carried in the blood — and he carries Dominate. The spider work I took for Cling of the Insect.

## Connections

- **[[Dutch]]** — his ghoul. Nearly died under [[Mitch]]'s frenzy at the clinic.
- **Clinic staff** — [[Antonio]], [[Lorena]], [[Victor]].
- **His herd** — the clinic's clientele, gang-adjacent. The emergency feeding cost him part of it.
- **[[Megan]]** — on the blackmail list because of him; he reached her by phone, anxious but safe.
- **The coterie** — [[Claire Evangelista Marte|me]], [[Mitch]], [[Spider]], [[Collin]].

## Notes

The first night cost him: [[Mitch]]'s frenzy wrecked the clinic and left a breach he called a problem deferred, not solved — drugged witnesses, paid-off staff, security smoothed over with Compel. His thing with Mitch started bad. *Don't talk. You don't talk. Get in the car.*

At the [[Darkhour Office]] he stopped mid-search to save the life of a man [[Collin]] had shot — hands in a dying man's blood, and he chose not to feed.

He tracked [[Jonathan]] by Blood Sorcery, burning blood-soaked gauze to raise a trail and following it through [[Garfield Park]] — feeding on [[Officer Williams]] under Dominate on the way — to the [[Hercules House]] gate, where he answered the riddle at the intercom: *three to five, it depends.*

At the house he refused to kill anyone all night, even as his poison failed again and again. He talked the knife out of [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]'s hand with a confession instead of a power — naming the night's dead and saying *I'm sorry about what happened to Michelle* — and carried the plea to the Prince that spared the family. Mid-fight he drove his poison scalpel into [[Mitch]]'s neck to try to break the Beast's hold on him, and after, made himself Mitch's support against it: *be here with the people who are willing to be here for you.*
