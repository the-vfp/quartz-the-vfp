# Darkhour Security

> On paper: a local security company. In practice: a hired cutout — surveillance muscle for the man behind the blackmail.

## Profile

Small operation. Bare-bones website. Phone goes straight to voicemail. Online security is robust enough to resist [[Collin]]'s people. The [[Darkhour Office]] in Ravenswood is freshly inhabited, mid-renovation, lived in maybe a couple of weeks — though the company on paper has existed longer.

The banking trail from [[Raphael Marte]]'s investigation in [[Journal — Session 0]] led here. Whether Darkhour is the principal behind the blackmail or a cutout is still open.

## Known Members

- **[[Steve Jensen]]** — dead, drained by [[Spider]] at the [[Darkhour Office]].
- **Charger driver** — female, late 20s, Eastern European features, hair up in a bun, wide-eyed (not a cool head). Drove a black Charger with a corporate fleet plate. Escaped from the [[Darkhour Office]] in [[Session 2]]. Fleet plate trackable.
- **Stairwell shooter** — unnamed, unconscious, stabilized. Shot twice by [[Collin]] in the stairwell at [[Darkhour Office]]; saved by [[Trip]]. Out for days. Interrogation asset if the coterie can move him.
- **Gate runner** — later named **[[Jim]]** (also Clarence/Lance). Juked himself into the fence after [[Collin]]'s called shot at his legs and knocked himself out on it. Interrogated in [[Session 3]] and released; knew nothing beyond the client's handle.
- **Voicemail caller** — female, used a voice changer, delivered a rehearsed script demanding monthly tribute on [[Raphael]]'s cell in [[Journal — Session 0]]. Different person from the Charger driver by voice pattern.
- **"Crawlspace lady"** — unnamed, mortal (was up during daylight). Planted the surveillance camera in [[Carter's Apartment]] that the coterie recovered in [[Session 1]]. On footage she crawled out of a wall during the day, seemed not to know what had happened, went back into the closet/crawlspace.
- **Blonde man** — unnamed in [[Session 1]]; big, blonde, tribal neck tattoos, hoodie. Beat [[Carter]] to find out where [[Mitch]] sleeps. Possibly the same person as [[Steve Jensen]] — description overlaps; identification not formally made in-fiction.

## Notes

Produced the footage of [[Walter Nash]] being attacked. Had previously been looking for [[Spider]] for reasons the coterie hasn't connected yet. Ran a full stalker operation on [[Carter]] out of the office.

Three silhouettes in a parked car watched the office during the [[Session 2]] raid — whether they were Darkhour backup, a rival investigation, or the blackmailers watching their own trap is unresolved.

At least two distinct operators identifiable so far: the voice-changed female voicemail caller and the Charger driver read as different people by voice/demeanor. Darkhour has more than one hand on the wheel.

**[[Session 3]]–[[Session 4]] resolution:** Darkhour turned out to be hired hands, not the principal. Their anonymous client — *Redwood*, later unmasked as **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]** — bought them the [[Darkhour Office]] and paid them to watch the coterie and ship footage. [[Steve Jensen]], the only operative who ever met the client in person, is dead; the gate runner [[Jim]] was released knowing nothing; the burner line went dead the moment Jensen did. As an organization, Darkhour is a spent lead — the trail ran *through* it to Paul.
