# verumtenebris.org

> Ancient, GeoCities-era conspiracy forum. Surfaced as a lead in [[Journal — Session 0]].

## Profile

Super old crackpot forum. Most of the image-heavy posts are by a single user, [[Dark Descent]] — blurry UFO photos, blurry Loch Ness photos, "werewolf at the harbor" — mixed in with thoroughly mundane posts like "show me your cat photos."

## Known Members

- **[[Dark Descent]]** — prolific poster.

## Notes

[[Raphael Marte]] ran the lead down between [[Journal — Session 0]] and [[Session 1]]. Called it a dead end. Still one of the three outstanding leads at the top of [[Session 2]] — Honored Drinks, Darkhour, or verumtenebris — and was deferred in favor of the physical evidence at the [[Darkhour Office]].

The forum poster [[Dark Descent]] and the blackmail-email signature *Redwood* were the same man — **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]**, unmasked in [[Session 4]]. That makes this forum a genuine trail in hindsight rather than crackpot noise: his hoard of catalogued werewolf and vampire blood squares with the werewolf-at-the-harbor post. The coterie never revisited the site on-screen — an open thread into Story 2.
