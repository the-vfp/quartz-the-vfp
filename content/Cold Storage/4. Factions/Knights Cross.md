# Knights Cross

> An up-and-coming gang of vampire hunters. Dagger through a skull.

## Profile

A relatively new, unaffiliated Chicago gang — not ordinary criminals, but a crew of **vampire hunters**. Identified by [[Trip]] (Streetwise) off the insignia on their armor: **a dagger driven through a skull**. They operate in open violation of the Masquerade. Not government-backed and not the Second Inquisition — but they possess real, factual knowledge of how to find and kill Kindred.

## Known Members

- Unnamed cell hired by [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]] for the [[Hercules House]] ambush — plate carriers, balaclavas, suppressed automatic weapons, wooden stakes, a nail bomb. **At least one member was female** — likely the "exhausted, annoyed" voice the coterie overheard arguing inside the house from the gate in [[Session 3]]. Four killed, one left alive (broken leg), **one fled**.

## Notes

First encountered in [[Session 4]], waiting in the rafters of the [[Hercules House]] as the final defense of Paul Sterry's trap. Camera-guided, disciplined, coordinated ("Concentrate fire," "Fire in the hole"). The coterie wiped out the cell but **one member escaped out the door** — a loose end who now knows this coterie exists and can kill hunters. As an organization with anti-Kindred knowledge operating in Chicago, they're a standing Masquerade problem beyond this one job.
