# Bridgepoint Consulting

> Legitimate community engagement consultancy co-owned by [[Claire Evangelista Marte|Claire]] and [[Raphael Marte|Raphael]]. Also Claire's Resources (•••) source.

## Profile

Real, operating business. Both a cover and a source of real income. The blackmailer in [[Journal — Session 0]] demanded a monthly wire equal to 25% of Bridgepoint's income — a sustained tap, not a payout — which indicates the blackmailer understands how much Bridgepoint brings in.

## Known Members

- **[[Claire Evangelista Marte]]** — co-owner.
- **[[Raphael Marte]]** — co-owner.

## Notes

The blackmail demand came via an email to Raphael's *work* address — a Bridgepoint address, not a public one. Whoever targeted them knew the internal channel to use.
