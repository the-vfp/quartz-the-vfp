# Carter

> [[Mitch]]'s mortal brother. Trapeze performer. Leverage everyone wants.

## Profile

Lives in apartment 802 of an eight-story building — the one where the coterie first met in [[Session 1]]. Surname "Marshall" surfaced in [[Session 2]].

## Connections

- **[[Mitch]]** — brother.
- **[[Dutch]]** — brought him to the clinic after the beating.

## Notes

Beaten in [[Session 1]] by a large blonde man — the assailant wanted to know where Mitch sleeps. Carter refused. Broken nose, hairline fracture in the orbital bone, fracture in the humerus above the elbow. Non-life-threatening, but the arm break means the trapeze artist is out of work for a long time.

Unconscious through the frenzy at [[Trip's Clinic]], spattered with blood.

[[Session 2]] established the blackmailers were running a full stalker operation on him out of the [[Darkhour Office]] — a wall of surveillance photos, printed schedule notes: *laundry 8–10:30pm*, *home by 4pm*, *take liberties red bike*.

Offscreen for [[Session 2]], recovering at [[Trip's Clinic]] with [[Mitch]] watching over him. Between sessions [[Mitch]] moved him out of the clinic to a safehouse with one of his work-friends. With the *Power Prey* operation dismantled in [[Session 4]] — the blackmailer unmade, the list resolved — the immediate threat to him is over, though [[Trip]] still jokes about billing for the treatment.
