# Paul Sterry Sr.

> *"Redwood."* A grieving mortal father who built an operation to make vampires hurt. Unmasked in [[Session 4]].

## Profile

A middle-aged man — thick glasses, thinning hair, slightly portly. For three sessions he was only a name signed at the bottom of blackmail emails and a calm voice in riddles. [[Session 4]] put a face on him: **Paul Sterry Sr.**, the father of **[[Michelle Sterry]]**, the fifteen-year-old [[Walter Nash]] fed on and killed. *Redwood* was always just a handle.

A mortal who figured out vampires are real and turned his grief into a campaign — *"You feel powerless… I can take from you just like you take from me. You took my Michelle."*

## Connections

- **[[Michelle Sterry]]** — his daughter; her death is the engine of everything he did.
- **[[Walter Nash]]** — killed Michelle; Paul stole Nash's vitae (via a captive ghoul) to power his own operation.
- **[[Jonathan]]** — held him hostage at knifepoint in the hidden office; released him under [[Trip]]'s plea.
- **[[Knights Cross]]** — the vampire-hunter gang he hired for the [[Hercules House]] ambush.
- **[[Darkhour Security]]** — the hired surveillance crew he ran as *Redwood*; provided their building.
- **[[Kevin Jackson]]** — the Prince who spared him and repurposed him.

## Notes

A one-man Masquerade breach: a dining room of catalogued, labeled jars of vampire **and werewolf** blood, files on where Kindred's loved ones live, ghoul dogs maintained from the vein, and a starved ghoul of Nash's padlocked in the basement as his vitae source. He had blackmailed **other Kindred** beyond this coterie.

He is also **[[Dark Descent]]**, the prolific [[verumtenebris.org]] poster whose "werewolf at the harbor" post the coterie had written off as crank noise — confirmation he'd been hunting the supernatural for real long before any of this. *Dark Descent* was the obsessive's forum face; *Redwood* was the operator's blackmail signature.

Resisted [[Claire]]'s Dominate outright — prepared, hard-minded, and openly aware: *"Don't try your tricks. I know what you are."* What broke him wasn't a power but [[Trip]] naming the night's dead and saying *"I'm sorry about what happened to Michelle."*

**Disposition:** Prince [[Kevin Jackson]] spared Paul and his family on the coterie's plea, relocated the wife and son into an ally's keeping, and kept Paul alive as an asset — pointing his investigative skills at the **[[Sabbat]] in Milwaukee**. He claims **dead-man's-switch drives** that release everything if anyone but him erases them; the coterie watched him delete *something* but couldn't confirm the data is truly gone. He remains, in their words, *theirs.*
