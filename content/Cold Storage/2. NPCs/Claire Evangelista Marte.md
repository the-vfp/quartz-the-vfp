# Claire Evangelista Marte

> Banu Haqim neonate. Librarian by training, fieldworker by circumstance.

## Profile

MLIS background. Spare and direct in speech. Gets very still and very focused when a situation needs action rather than dramatic. Embraced roughly ten years ago by [[Aicha Rana (Sire)|Aicha Rana]]. Frames her Blood Sorcery as information retrieval translated into a supernatural medium — blood as data to be parsed, not mysticism to be performed.

Predator type: Pursuer. Sect: Camarilla.

## Connections

- **[[Raphael Marte]]** — husband, Touchstone, Ally (•••/••). Knows what she is. Currently at a safehouse with the daughters conducting parallel investigation.
- **[[Holly Marte]]** and **[[Joy Marte]]** — daughters, ~19 and ~17. Do *not* know.
- **[[Aicha Rana (Sire)|Aicha Rana]]** — sire. Established narratively.
- **Coterie:** [[Trip]], [[Mitch]], [[Spider]], [[Collin]]. Brought together under duress via shared blackmail.

## Notes

Across the *Power Prey* arc she did the fieldwork while [[Raphael Marte|Raphael]] worked the financial trail: spotted the watchers outside the [[Darkhour Office]] and chased the fleeing runner ([[Session 2]]); read the captured operatives' blood and talked [[Collin]] down from a frenzy with leadership rather than gentleness ([[Session 3]]); and in [[Session 4]] took a ghoul dog's teeth to the throat, intimidated a hunter into fleeing rather than fight, found the hidden office by ear, and argued against trading [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]'s surviving family for political credit. Carries one less Humanity than the rest of the coterie by predator type.
