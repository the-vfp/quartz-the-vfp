# Collin

> Brujah. The only coterie member who was not personally blackmailed.

## Profile

Was independently investigating [[Walter Nash]] when the rest of the coterie revealed they were being extorted. Communicates via handwritten notes in his office. Competent with firearms — the rest of the coterie clocked in [[Session 2]] that this wasn't his first firefight.

His people investigated [[Darkhour Security]] early on. The company's online security resisted whatever they could throw at it.

## Connections

- **[[Jonathan]]** — touchstone; like a brother, raised together. Kidnapped to force Collin into the coterie's situation, and recovered in [[Session 4]].
- **Coterie:** [[Claire Evangelista Marte|Claire]], [[Trip]], [[Mitch]], [[Spider]].

## Notes

In [[Session 2]] he dropped the lead assailant through the front door with a called shot, then put a called shot at the legs of the fleeing runner — the runner juked wrong and drove his own forehead into the gate. Alive, unconscious, dropped at the coterie's feet.

In [[Session 3]] he scrubbed his shop's own surveillance to learn [[Jonathan]] had walked out under his own power (*I'm not this stupid*), then took the kidnapper's distorted *tick tock* call and gave in to a **fury frenzy** — smashing the phone to pieces before [[Spider]] and [[Mitch]] grappled him and [[Claire]] put her face in his and refused to let him fall.

In [[Session 4]] he would not wait outside the gate — *my friend is in there* — ran the railing dual-wielding on Celerity, and when a hunter asked what they were, answered *just vampire hunters.* He recovered [[Jonathan]] alive from the hidden office: the one clean win of the night.
