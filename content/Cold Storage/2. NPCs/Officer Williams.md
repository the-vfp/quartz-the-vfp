# Officer Williams

> A city bylaw officer in the wrong park on the wrong night.

## Profile

City bylaw officer working [[Garfield Park]]. Otherwise unremarkable — in the wrong place when [[Trip]] needed to feed.

## Connections

- **[[Trip]]** — fed on him.

## Notes

In [[Session 3]], [[Trip]] dominated him under the cover of testing "sugar pills," took three dots, set him on a bench, and called *assistance needed* on the man's own radio before walking back to the car. Clean by the rules of the [[Garfield Park]] Elysium (*clean up your mess, but all are free to feed*) — but he'll wake in a hospital with a story he can't explain. Loose end.

[[Trip]] counted him by name in [[Session 4]] among the people he'd hurt that night — the guilt stuck.
