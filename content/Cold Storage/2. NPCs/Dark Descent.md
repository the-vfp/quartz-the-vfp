# Dark Descent

> Prolific [[verumtenebris.org]] user — the crackpot-forum persona of [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]].

## Profile

Posts blurry UFO photos, blurry Loch Ness Monster photos, a post about seeing "a werewolf at the harbor." Image posts are almost all theirs. Mixed in among completely mundane posts — "show me your cat photos" alongside the crackpot material.

[[Raphael Marte]] ran down the forum lead between [[Journal — Session 0]] and [[Session 1]]. Called it a dead end for now.

## Notes

The werewolf-at-the-harbor post is the standout. Everything else could be hobbyist paranoid; that one is harder to dismiss given what the coterie now knows exists.

*Dark Descent* and *Redwood* are the same man — **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]**, unmasked in [[Session 4]]. *Dark Descent* was his crackpot-forum persona; *Redwood* was his blackmail alias. The werewolf-at-the-harbor post squares exactly with the dining room of catalogued **werewolf** and vampire blood found in his house: he was never a crank, but a grieving father who had been hunting the supernatural for real for years. The forum the coterie dismissed as noise was a genuine trail the whole time.
