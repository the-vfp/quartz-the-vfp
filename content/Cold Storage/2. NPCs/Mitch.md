# Mitch

> Gangrel. Brother to [[Carter]].

## Profile

Animalistic features surface when the Beast rises — elongated ears and arms, hands becoming dagger-like claws. Disciplines: Feral Weapons, Fortitude.

## Connections

- **[[Carter]]** — mortal brother. Trapeze performer. The leverage everyone else wants.
- **[[Trip]]** and **[[Spider]]** — the two who keep pulling him back from the edge; his support against the Beast.
- **Coterie:** [[Claire Evangelista Marte|Claire]], [[Trip]], [[Spider]], [[Collin]].

## Notes

In [[Session 1]], Mitch frenzied at the news of Carter's beating and tore into [[Dutch]] at [[Trip's Clinic]] — five dagger-like fingernails into the upper torso, whole health tracker filled with superficial in a single strike. Came back to himself to find the clinic wrecked, a cowering nurse, ten witnesses, and [[Trip]]'s silence.

One stain on Humanity. Passed the remorse check — did not lose a point.

Offscreen for [[Session 2]], tending to [[Carter]] at the clinic. Between sessions he moved Carter out of [[Trip's Clinic]] to a safehouse with one of Carter's work-friends.

In [[Session 3]] he arrived at the [[Darkhour Office]] on foot, nearly run down by the fleeing Charger driver; read the captured [[Jim]] well enough to put his scalpel away, realizing the man genuinely didn't grasp how badly Carter had been hurt; helped [[Spider]] grapple [[Collin]] out of a frenzy; and at the [[Hercules House]] gate caught what the others missed — *those aren't dogs* — clocking the shepherds as ghouls before [[Trip]] crossed the threshold.

In [[Session 4]] he caught a spring-loaded stake-rake on his forearms (heart unpierced) and kept it as a weapon, then took a messy critical into a **Harm Compulsion** — the Beast's certainty that every hunter in the house dies tonight. The kill it forced cost him a **second Stain on Humanity**. He's openly struggling now: each frenzy leaves a *permanent* physical change (rare even for a Gangrel), and he asked [[Trip]] for help out loud. *I owe you.*
