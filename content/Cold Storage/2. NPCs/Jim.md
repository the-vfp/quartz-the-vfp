# Jim

> The gate runner. A surveillance gigworker who knew nothing. Let go.

## Profile

Gave his name as Jim, then Clarence, then Lance, then back to Jim. Took the pictures of [[Carter]]; didn't make the calls and didn't know the operation's shape. Read by [[Claire]] as fleeting Melancholic. Kept describing the beating of Carter as *a couple of love taps* — and [[Mitch]], reading him, realized the man genuinely didn't know "love taps" meant a hospitalization.

## Connections

- **[[Darkhour Security]]** — hired gig labor, bottom of the chain. Knew [[Redwood]] only as a name.
- **[[Steve Jensen]]** — the one who freelanced the actual beating; Jim was only in the car.

## Notes

Knee'd [[Claire]] in the temple fleeing the [[Darkhour Office]] in [[Session 2]], then knocked himself out on the gate. Woke up in [[Session 3]] and talked. [[Claire]] took two dots of hunger off him, clean against her Convictions. He played a panicked call to Redwood's burner (drawing on a middle-school *Pirates of Penzance* run as a tree) — the line was already dead.

Released. [[Collin]] put a pistol to his temple to make the cost of return clear; [[Trip]] handed him pills, which he dropped in the first bush past the gate before vanishing. If he turns up dead within a week, [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]] is cleaning up loose ends.

[[Trip]] named him again in [[Session 4]] — *Clarence, with multiple head wounds* — in his tally of the people he'd hurt that night.
