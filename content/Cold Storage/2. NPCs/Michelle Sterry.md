# Michelle Sterry

> The grief at the root of everything. [[Walter Nash]]'s victim. [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]'s daughter.

## Profile

A fifteen-year-old girl. [[Walter Nash]] fed on her and dumped her in a park; she lay comatose for over a month before she died. Never appears in life — she is the absence the entire campaign was built around.

## Connections

- **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]** — her father, who built the whole blackmail operation out of his grief over her.
- **[[Walter Nash]]** — killed her.

## Notes

Revealed in [[Session 4]] as the answer to the riddle her father posed at the gate — *"how many pints can a 110-pound 15-year-old girl lose before she becomes irrevocably brain-damaged?"* The "Power Prey" arc resolves to this: a mortal father who lost his daughter to a vampire and turned that loss into a weapon. Her death is the in-fiction reason the Prince took the coterie's case against Nash seriously.
