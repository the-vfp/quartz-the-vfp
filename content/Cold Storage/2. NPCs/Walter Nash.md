# Walter Nash

> Central to the blackmail situation. Killed a child, and the coterie now holds a Blood Hunt on him.

## Profile

Unknown directly. What the coterie has on him is indirect — he appears on [[Darkhour Security]]-produced footage being attacked. The attacker in that footage was [[Steve Jensen]], wearing clothing branded with [[Honored Drinks]].

## Connections

- **[[Darkhour Security]]** — produced the footage of him.
- **[[Steve Jensen]]** — attacked him on camera.
- **[[Honored Drinks]]** — branding on Jensen's clothing in the footage.
- **[[Spider]]** — sent by a superior to investigate Nash.
- **[[Collin]]** — was independently investigating Nash before being pulled into the coterie.

## Notes

Details from the unrecorded first half of [[Session 1]] are missing. The footage seems to show him feeding.

**[[Session 4]] reveal:** the "feeding" was the killing of **[[Michelle Sterry]]**, a fifteen-year-old he fed on and dumped in a park (comatose over a month before she died). Her father, **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]**, built the entire blackmail operation out of that grief. Nash's stolen vitae fed Paul's ghoul dogs, and one of Nash's own ghouls was the starved captive in the [[Hercules House]] basement, to be returned to him.

At the close of [[Session 4]], Prince [[Kevin Jackson]] granted the coterie the **right of first refusal on a Blood Hunt against Nash** — explicitly flagged as the bridge into Story 2.
