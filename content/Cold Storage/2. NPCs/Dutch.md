# Dutch

> [[Trip]]'s ghoul. Nearly killed in [[Session 1]].

## Profile

Was present as an "observer only" when [[Carter]] was beaten — the attackers wanted someone to carry the message back about how serious they were. Brought Carter to [[Trip's Clinic]] afterward.

## Connections

- **[[Trip]]** — domitor.
- **[[Carter]]** — observed his beating; escorted him to care.

## Notes

In [[Session 1]], [[Mitch]] frenzied on the news Dutch delivered — the Beast's logic was that Dutch had been present and didn't protect Carter, so Dutch was the target. Five dagger-like fingernails into his upper torso, neck, and collarbone. Whole health tracker filled with superficial in a single strike. Took aggravated damage from a second blow before the frenzy broke.

Severely wounded but alive. Watched over Carter after the meeting in [[Session 1]] while the coterie slept.
