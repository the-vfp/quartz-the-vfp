# Trip

> Tremere. Runs a private medical clinic with an apartment above it.

## Profile

Clinic owner. Pragmatic, damage-control reflex. Outed as a warlock to the coterie in [[Session 2]] when he performed a ritual with a glass vial and a single spider on approach to the Darkhour office.

Disciplines include Blood Sorcery (Scorpion's Touch) and Dominate (Compel). Also demonstrated ritual work ([[Session 2]]) that the group read as Cling of the Insect.

## Connections

- **[[Dutch]]** — ghoul. Brought [[Carter]] to the clinic in [[Session 1]] and was nearly killed by [[Mitch]]'s frenzy.
- **Clinic staff:** [[Antonio]], [[Lorena]], [[Victor]].
- **Herd** — clinic clientele, including gang-affiliated patients. Lost one dot temporarily after emergency feeding during the [[Session 1]] masquerade breach.
- **[[Megan]]** — on the blackmail list because of him; he checked on her by phone in [[Session 3]] (alive, anxious).
- **Coterie:** [[Claire Evangelista Marte|Claire]], [[Mitch]], [[Spider]], [[Collin]].

## Notes

[[Session 1]]'s frenzy cascade left a masquerade breach — "problem deferred, not solved." Drugged witnesses, paid-off staff, Compel-managed security. Will come back.

Relationship with [[Mitch]] is strained from the start. "Don't fucking talk. You don't fucking talk. Get in the car."

In [[Session 2]] he stopped mid-search to save the life of an assailant [[Collin]] had shot — hands in a dying man's blood and chose not to feed.

In [[Session 3]] he tracked [[Jonathan]] by Blood Sorcery, burning a blood-soaked gauze to raise a scent-trail and following it through [[Garfield Park]] — feeding on [[Officer Williams]] under Dominate in the Conservatory Elysium — to the [[Hercules House]] gate, where he answered the intercom riddle: *three to five, it depends.*

In [[Session 4]] he refused to kill anyone all night, even as his Scorpion's Touch poison failed contest after contest. He talked the knife out of [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]'s hand with a confession rather than a power — naming the night's dead and saying *I'm sorry about what happened to Michelle* — then carried the final plea to Prince [[Kevin Jackson]] that spared the Sterry family. Mid-combat he drove a poison scalpel into [[Mitch]]'s neck trying to break a Harm Compulsion, and afterward stepped in as Mitch's support against the Beast: *be here with the people who are willing to be here for you.*
