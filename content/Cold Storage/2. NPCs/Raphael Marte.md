# Raphael Marte

> Claire's husband. Touchstone. Ally (•••/••). Partner and parallel investigator.

## Profile

Knows what [[Claire Evangelista Marte|Claire]] is and has known for years. Co-owner of [[Bridgepoint Consulting]], which is both a legitimate community engagement consultancy and Claire's Resources. Cooks. Keeps the santoku sharp.

Hits the wall when the news is bad, and then comes back fast and starts working. In [[Journal — Session 0]]'s journal, Claire walked in on him holding the knife badly and phone-in-the-other-hand; by the end of the night he was running bank routing numbers at the kitchen table.

## Connections

- **[[Claire Evangelista Marte|Claire]]** — wife.
- **[[Holly Marte]]** and **[[Joy Marte]]** — daughters. He is the one keeping them safe and in the dark.

## Notes

Currently at the Marte safehouse with the girls, working the financial trail on [[Darkhour Security]] while Claire does fieldwork. Found the banking routing that led to Darkhour in [[Journal — Session 0]] and ran down the [[verumtenebris.org]] lead before [[Session 1]] — turning up the [[Dark Descent]] user.

[[Claire Evangelista Marte|Claire]] phoned him during [[Session 3]] and confirmed no change in his situation — safe at the safehouse. His was the second name on the recovered blackmail list; with the operation dismantled in [[Session 4]], the threat that put him there is gone.

Never flatten the relationship into protector/protected. They think in tandem.
