# Spider

> Nosferatu.

## Profile

Sent by a superior to investigate [[Walter Nash]]. Has prior history with [[Darkhour Security]] — they were looking for *him* at one point, which may or may not connect to the current situation.

## Connections

- **Coterie:** [[Claire Evangelista Marte|Claire]], [[Trip]], [[Mitch]], [[Collin]].

## Notes

In [[Session 2]], Spider charged the stairwell at the Darkhour office, took three bullets to the midsection, ripped the gas mask off the man at the top, and drained him dry. The man was [[Steve Jensen]].

This was his first kill. He broke his own conviction — no feeding on living mortals — to get there. Session ended with him on all fours over Jensen's body, asking [[Trip]] *is he dead? did I fuck up?*

Asked [[Trip]] about a blood bag hookup in [[Session 1]] — that conversation never happened, given what followed.

In [[Session 3]] he sat with that first kill and held himself together — passing the Humanity check without loss — then climbed the [[Hercules House]] brick on Uncanny Grip and helped grapple [[Collin]] down from his frenzy. To [[Mitch]], another monster having a bad night, he said it plainly: *I got your back.*

In [[Session 4]] he came into his own as the coterie's heavy: a Potence-driven blow took a ghoul dog's head clean off, he walked a hunter's rifle into the man's own ally (breaking his Obfuscate to do it), and he hurled another hunter bodily off the balcony. He ended the night out of Willpower and riding a feeding compulsion.
