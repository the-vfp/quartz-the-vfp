# Aicha Rana (Banu Haqim)

[[Khadija]]'s loyal childe, fixer, and guide. Aicha, socially and economically speaking, could not be more different from her sire. She carries herself and speaks as if a life of poverty and survival and simple joys are all she's ever known, and appears to be among the rarest things a kindred can be: an optimist. She is learning the ins and outs of Chicago rather quickly, given her and her sire's relatively recent arrival, and has done only an okay job of keeping quiet about her unfavorable opinion of most local kindred and their "moral turpitude," at least amongst other children of Haqim.
