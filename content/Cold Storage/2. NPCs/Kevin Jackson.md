# Kevin Jackson

> Prince of Chicago. The authority the coterie answered to at the end of "Power Prey."

## Profile

The Camarilla Prince of Chicago. Heard the coterie's case at the close of [[Session 4]] with real interest — particularly the parts touching [[Walter Nash]].

## Connections

- **[[Camarilla]]** — sits at the top of the Chicago court.
- **The coterie** — now on his radar as a group that can handle delicate problems and survive a hunter ambush as near-strangers to his court.
- **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]** — spared on the coterie's plea and kept as an asset.

## Notes

His instinct was to have [[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]] killed in front of him. [[Trip]]'s final plea — the whole coterie behind it — turned him. He **spared the Sterry family**, relocated them into an ally's keeping, and repurposed Paul against the **[[Sabbat]] in Milwaukee**.

Rewarded the coterie with his attention and the **right of first refusal on a Blood Hunt against [[Walter Nash]]**. First named in [[Session 4]]; likely a recurring authority figure as the chronicle shifts to clan/sect politics in Story 2.
