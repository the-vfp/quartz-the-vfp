# Jonathan

> [[Collin]]'s touchstone. The fifth name on the blackmail list. Taken — and recovered.

## Profile

Works a transaction-counter at a shop tied to [[Collin]] — handles customers, knows the regulars. On [[Collin]]'s surveillance footage he took a call from Collin about online research, opened his laptop, then visibly caught himself: *I'm not this stupid. I have to get off this IP.* He closed the laptop, took it with him, locked up, and walked out under his own power.

## Connections

- **[[Collin]]** — touchstone. Collin had not been blackmailed earlier because Jonathan had not yet been taken; the night [[Walter Nash]] called in his favors and Collin showed up unsolicited, his office wasn't set up for the first time in his standing arrangement.
- **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]** — the captor. Held Jonathan hooded and tied in the [[Hercules House]] hidden office, a Bowie knife to his throat, until [[Trip]] talked him down in [[Session 4]].

## Notes

His name was on the **blackmail list** sticky note recovered at the [[Darkhour Office]] in [[Session 3]] — alongside [[Carter]], [[Raphael]], Megan, and Gladys Ackerman. The list resolved the structural question of the campaign: every coterie member was blackmailed because someone they loved was on it.

[[Trip]] tracked him by Blood Sorcery — the trail ran through [[Garfield Park]] (where he'd been moved) and terminated inside the [[Hercules House]]. He walked out of Collin's shop on his own feet, so wherever he is now, he got there before he was taken.

**[[Session 4]]:** found hooded, taped, and tied to a chair in the [[Hercules House]] hidden office, held at knifepoint by **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]**. Recovered alive — [[Collin]] kept the hood on him so he wouldn't see the coterie at work and moved him to safety. The one clean win of the night.
