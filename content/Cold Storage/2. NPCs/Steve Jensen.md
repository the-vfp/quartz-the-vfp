# Steve Jensen

> The man on the Nash footage. Drained dry by [[Spider]] at the top of the stairs. Dead.

## Profile

Tall white man, blonde. In the [[Walter Nash]] footage he wore a jacket branded with [[Honored Drinks]]. ID number 633CH.

## Connections

- **[[Darkhour Security]]** — the footage of him attacking Nash was produced there; he was at the [[Darkhour Office]] in [[Session 2]].
- **[[Walter Nash]]** — attacked him on camera.

## Notes

The Blonde Man who beat [[Carter]] in [[Session 1]] matched this description — big blonde, hoodie, tribal tattoos on the neck — but the identification was not formally made in-fiction. Open question whether "Blonde Man who beat Carter" and "Steve Jensen" are the same person, or two members of the same outfit.

In [[Session 2]], Jensen was at the top of the stairs at the [[Darkhour Office]] with a gas mask and a gun. [[Spider]] charged him, took three bullets, wrenched the gun aside, ripped the mask off, and drained him completely. Spider broke his own conviction against feeding on living mortals to do it.

In [[Session 3]] his body rode in [[Trip]]'s trunk, to be called in later as a John Doe — and the coterie learned he had been the **only [[Darkhour Security]] operative ever to meet the client in person**. The ~7-minute call on his burner was that client; the line went dead the moment Jensen did. [[Session 4]] confirmed the client was **[[Redwood|Paul Sterry Sr.]]**, and named Jensen again among the night's dead in [[Trip]]'s confession.
