## Session Summary (Recorded Portion)

### Between Nights — Downtime

The session picked up with the coterie handling end-of-night business and waking up the next evening:

**[[Collin]]** checked in (via text) saying the Nash situation was being worked on with "plenty of leads" and would be resolved before the deadline. [[Collin]]'s investigation into [[Darkhour Security|Dark Hour Security]] hit a wall — their online security was solid enough to resist whatever his people could throw at it.

**[[Claire Evangelista Marte|Claire]]** received a report from **Raphael** — a combination of voice notes and text messages. Raph found an ancient conspiracy theory forum (GeoCities-era) with a user called **"[[Dark Descent]]"** who posted blurry UFO photos, Loch Ness Monster pictures, and a post about seeing "a werewolf at the harbor" — mixed in with completely mundane posts like "show me your cat photos." Most of the image posts were by this single user. Dead end for now. [[Claire Evangelista Marte|Claire]] woke up alone in a dark, silent house with a "vestigial instinct" to put on coffee and toast that would do nothing for her.

**[[Spider]]** woke up feeling good, well-fed. Mentioned wanting to ask [[Trip]] about a blood bag hookup. Also remembered he'd previously had interactions with [[Darkhour Security|Dark Hour Security]] — they had been looking for _him_ at one point, which could be related to the current situation.

**[[Mitch]]** — see below.

### The Frenzy at the Clinic

This scene dominated the recorded session. What was supposed to be a quick scene became the entire session due to catastrophic dice rolls.

**Setup:** [[Mitch]] and [[Trip]] were staying at [[Trip]]'s place (apartment above the clinic). [[Dutch]], [[Trip]]'s ghoul, arrived at the door with news: **[[Carter]] had been beaten.** Broken nose, hairline fracture in the orbital bone, fracture in the humerus above the elbow. Non-life-threatening, but a broken arm means the trapeze artist is out of work for a long time.

[[Dutch]] reported: the attackers wanted to know **where [[Mitch]] sleeps** — that was the price they demanded. [[Carter]] refused to give up his brother's location, so they beat it out of him (or tried to). The attacker was described as a **big blonde man with tribal tattoos on his neck, wearing a hoodie**. [[Dutch]] was there as an observer only, but the attackers wanted him to "talk about how serious they are."

**The Frenzy Cascade:**

1. Tyler called for a **frenzy resist check** (fury frenzy) — [[Mitch]]'s Gangrel bane severity imposed a -2 dice penalty, dropping the pool from 5 to 3 at difficulty 3
2. [[Mitch]] **failed the resist**, spent willpower to reroll, and **failed again**
3. The Beast's logic: [[Dutch]] was present when [[Carter]] was beaten and didn't protect him → [[Dutch]] is the target
4. [[Mitch]]'s **Feral Weapons** activated — hands elongated into dagger-like claws, ears and arms lengthened, proportions went wrong. He turned toward [[Dutch]] with killing intent
5. [[Trip]] recognized the frenzy immediately (his clinic has dealt with this before) and called a **code** — clinic protocol for restraining violent patients
6. [[Trip]] grabbed a **scalpel**, cut his own palm to coat it with his Blood Sorcery-enhanced vitae (**Scorpion's Touch**), and drove it into [[Mitch]]'s shoulder
7. [[Mitch]]'s claws tore into [[Dutch]] — **five dagger-like fingernails** entering [[Dutch]]'s upper torso/neck/collarbone area. [[Dutch]]'s entire health tracker filled with superficial damage in one strike
8. [[Trip]]'s Scorpion's Touch was supposed to paralyze [[Mitch]], but it **only works on mortals** — useless against another Kindred
9. [[Trip]] attempted to **blood surge** his attack, failed the rouse check, gained more hunger, and fell under a **hunger compulsion**
10. [[Trip]] tried to bite [[Mitch]] (called shot at -2 dice), but was mathematically incapable of overpowering him physically — "I was counting on the three bruisers in the crew to handle most of that stuff"
11. [[Trip]] resorted to **spitting venom** (Dex-based attack) since he couldn't physically overpower [[Mitch]]
12. [[Mitch]] attempted another willpower check to resist — needed 3 successes on 2 dice (only possible via critical). **Failed.**
13. Tyler narrated: _"Looking through your own eyes as if from the back seat of a seven-person minivan, you can see that you are ruining everything, but you cannot stop that thing that is driving you."_
14. [[Mitch]] dealt **aggravated damage** to [[Dutch]], then got a third willpower check with a bonus die — needed 3 successes on 3 dice. **Succeeded.** The frenzy broke.
15. [[Mitch]] came back to himself with his fingernails physically inside [[Dutch]]'s body, [[Trip]]'s venom in his shoulder, blood everywhere, [[Carter]] unconscious and spattered with blood, a cowering nurse, and a room full of witnesses.

**The Aftermath:**

- [[Trip]] went into **damage control mode** — his clinic, his problem
- He used one dot of **Herd** (temporarily, for the duration of the story) to feed on a patient, dragging a gang-affiliated client to a back room under the cover of treating an "injury"
- The feeding was a **messy critical** — he succeeded in not killing the patient (Resolve + Medicine check), but the Beast demanded more. [[Trip]] injected the patient with something, drained him further, and was seen by arriving security staff doing it
- [[Trip]] used **Compel** (Dominate) to manage the remaining witnesses
- Some staff were paid off. Others were drugged. The situation was "not a problem solved, but a problem deferred" — drugged witnesses will wake up wondering if they hallucinated it, and others got a fat payday to keep quiet
- [[Trip]] fed one more hunger point off the drugged patient
- **~10 people** witnessed some or all of what happened — a serious **Masquerade breach** contained but not resolved
- [[Mitch]] received a **stain on his Humanity**

**The Remorse Check:**

Tyler narrated [[Mitch]]'s inner monologue during the silent car ride with a furious [[Trip]]:

> _"This is the reason you feel the way you feel about the person that made you this way, about the monster that made you this way. This is the reason you haven't tried to get into being okay with this world. This is almost as bad as things could be. You were lucky to bring it back, but maybe you're not cut out for this. Maybe you are not a good enough person to be a good monster."_

[[Mitch]] rolled **remorse** — 2 dice, needed 1 success. **Succeeded.** His own voice answered: _"If you were a monster, if you were so awful, surely you wouldn't be thinking this hard about this."_ He did not lose a point of Humanity.

**[[Mitch]]'s last line before getting in the car:** "So that's me." **[[Trip]]'s response:** "Don't fucking talk. You don't fucking talk. Get in the car."

They arrived at the meeting point roughly an hour late. [[Mitch]] was wearing [[Trip]]'s ill-fitting clothes, his own being blood-soaked.

## Session End

The session ended with the coterie gathered outside the apartment building, [[Mitch]] and [[Trip]] arriving late and visibly worse for wear. Tyler noted this was "supposed to be a very quick scene" that spiraled due to the dice.

Post-session discussion revealed:

- This was the group's first exposure to frenzy mechanics: "I was wondering how that frenzy would work." Tyler: "That was the best possible way to demonstrate that the beast is not your friend."
- [[Mitch]]'s player on the lesson learned: "If I know I'm prone to frenzy, do I go check on my loved ones? Because what if I make it worse?"
- [[Spider]]'s player on [[Trip]]'s new dynamic with [[Mitch]]: "And now my new best friend [[Trip]] knows that too and will like help me make better choices." [[Trip]]'s player: "By which I mean I'm not admitting any of your friends and family."
- [[Collin]]'s player noted: "My only issue is that I don't really feel like I know [[Claire Evangelista Marte|Claire]] at all yet." The group reassured him it would come.
- Someone observed: "Maybe being bad at a vampire means you're a good person."
