# Session 2 — The Darkhour Office
Stream link: [https://youtube.com/live/OU8MJ_Wn3Ag](https://youtube.com/live/OU8MJ_Wn3Ag "https://youtube.com/live/OU8MJ_Wn3Ag")

## Session Summary

With the blackmailers' deadline closing in and [[Mitch]] offscreen moving [[Carter]] to safety at [[Trip]]'s clinic, the coterie converged outside [[Carter]]'s building and decided not to split up. [[Trip]] looked worse than the night before. Of the three leads available — the [[Honored Drinks]] bar, the [[Darkhour Security]] office in Ravenswood, and the [[verumtenebris.org]] trail — they chose Darkhour. Physical evidence over people, and with one member already gone for the night, splitting the party felt like a worse risk than ever.

[[Trip]] drove. Ravenswood was exactly what the word "desolate" gets used for — flickering lampposts, abandoned crane jobs, blinking blue security lights above nothing. On approach, [[Claire]]'s read of the building picked up the basics: two-story, no employee lot, blinds drawn, a fifteen-to-eighteen-foot solid steel fence, locked gate. [[Spider]], following his instincts rather than the building itself, spotted three silhouettes in a dark car across the street. Facing Darkhour. Watching it. Watching whoever showed up.

The coterie's read: either Darkhour's own people expecting trouble, or a third party running the same investigation. They chose to slip around the back rather than engage.

Before the climb, [[Trip]] pulled out a glass vial and a single spider and performed a ritual that visibly confirmed to the rest of the coterie what he is. Nobody looked surprised.

Getting over the fence was ugly. [[Spider]] went over almost effortlessly. [[Claire]], boosted from below and pulled from above, got up adequately. [[Collin]]'s first attempt failed so badly that [[Spider]]'s hands and [[Trip]]'s hands both "slipped" on him — suspiciously on purpose — and [[Claire]] unloaded on him for it. He made it over on the second try, gracelessly, under [[Trip]]'s silence.

Inside the perimeter, the property was wrong. No visible cameras. No discrete cameras either. No alarm panel. A window latch that popped open like a credit-card door for [[Spider]] — who realized mid-force that he'd snapped a latch that wasn't even locked. A light on the second floor had shut off as they'd rounded the back, so somebody was definitely in there. Nobody came out. The building read as recently-moved-into, mid-renovation, maybe a couple of weeks lived in, though the company on paper had existed longer.

Inside the front room: a CRT monitor rigged to a PC tower, a closed laptop, a jacket on a chair that looked like the jacket from the Nash attack footage, and one wall absolutely covered in surveillance photos of [[Carter]] — not candids, stalker work. Sticky notes next to the photos: _laundry 8–10:30pm_, _home by 4pm_, _take liberties red bike_. A printed email tucked under the monitor as a paperweight.

And that's when the tear gas came down the stairs.

[[Claire]] shut her senses off in time. Smoke filled the stairwell, a flashlight swept down from the landing, and voices called "Go, go, go" from both upstairs and outside. The coterie fractured into simultaneous actions.

[[Spider]] launched up the stairs straight into the flashlight. He grappled the assailant at the top, took three bullets into his midsection on the way in, wrenched the gun aside, ripped the gas mask off, and drained the man dry. The face under the mask, illuminated by the man's own flashlight: **[[Steve Jensen]]**. The name from the Nash footage. [[Spider]] broke his own conviction — no feeding on living mortals — to get there.

[[Collin]] took cover and positioned between [[Claire]] and the front door. When two more assailants kicked it open, he dropped the lead with a clean called shot; the second broke and ran.

[[Trip]], already working the papers on the table, pivoted when [[Collin]]'s second target went down in the stairwell shot twice in the chest. Instead of continuing the search, he went into emergency-response mode and saved the man's life. Stabilized him on the floor. Then, hands soaked in the blood of someone he'd just pulled back from dying, he held himself together and did not feed.

[[Claire]] chased the runner out the back. She grabbed the dropped baton, tried to trip him, failed, and took a knee to the temple for the trouble. She ducked out of the line of fire as the driver of a waiting black Charger opened up on the doorway.

[[Collin]] followed, split his fire between the fleeing runner and the driver covering him, and put a called shot at the runner's legs. The runner juked at the wrong moment and drove his own forehead into the edge of the gate — unconscious, alive, dropped at the coterie's feet. The driver hit the gas and was gone.

[[Claire]] caught the driver's description through her heightened senses: female, late 20s, Eastern European features, hair up in a bun. The Charger carried a corporate fleet plate — trackable.

End of session. [[Spider]] is still on all fours over Jensen's body, blood-soaked, caught in the edge of [[Trip]]'s flashlight. _"Is he dead? Did I fuck up?"_ Tyler called the scene there rather than run what comes next on the spot — it will open Session 3.
