The *All Plants* tab is your whole collection in one scrollable list — every plant you've added, living or resting. Two rows of buttons at the top let you reorder the list and choose what shows, so you can find one plant fast or just browse the shelf.

![[plant-tracker-all-plants.png|320]]

## Sorting your plants

The top row sets the order:

1. Open the *All Plants* tab
2. Tap *Date Acquired*, *Name*, or *Type*

- *Date Acquired* — the default; oldest first, so your longest-tended plants lead.
- *Name* — straight alphabetical.
- *Type* — groups the same species together (all your Pothos in a row), with untyped plants last.

## Filtering by status

The second row decides which plants appear:

1. Tap *All*, *Alive*, or *Resting*

- *Alive* — the default; your active collection only.
- *All* — living and resting together.
- *Resting* — only plants you've [[Putting a plant to rest|laid to rest]]. This is your graveyard view.

Because *Alive* is the default, resting plants stay hidden until you switch the filter — so the graveyard never clutters your everyday list.

> [!NOTE] Reading a plant row
> A living plant shows its type and when it was last watered (*Watered today*, *3 days ago*, *Never watered*), plus a 💧 button to water it on the spot. A resting plant shows a 🪦 and the date it went to rest instead.

## Adding a plant from here

The *+* button in the bottom-right corner appears only on this tab — tap it to [[Adding a plant|add a new plant]] to your collection.
