Not every plant that's gone needs to be erased. Laying a plant to rest moves it to the graveyard and stops it asking for care, but keeps everything you logged — its waterings, notes, and photos. It's the gentle alternative to [[Deleting a plant|deleting]], which wipes all of that for good.

> [!TIP] When in doubt, rest it
> Not sure whether to delete? Rest the plant instead — it's reversible, and you can still delete it later once you're certain.

## Laying a plant to rest

![[plant-tracker-agnes-page.png|320]]

1. Open the plant's page
2. Tap 🪦 *Lay to rest* in the header
3. When the prompt asks to confirm — for example, `Lay "Sally" to rest in the graveyard? Its history is kept, and you can revive it anytime.` — tap *OK*

The home dashboard reopens. Sally drops out of the *Tend* tab, the week-ahead strip, and the *to tend · total* count in the header — resting plants don't ask for care. Everything you logged stays intact, tucked into the graveyard.

## Finding plants in the graveyard

![[plant-tracker-all-plants-resting.png|320]]

Resting plants live under the *Resting* filter on the *All Plants* tab.

1. Open the *All Plants* tab
2. Tap *Resting*

Each resting plant shows a 🪦 and the date it went to rest (*Resting since Jun 3, 2026*). Open one to read back everything you logged while it was alive. If nothing's resting yet, you'll see "The graveyard is empty. 🌿". See [[Browsing all your plants]] for the rest of the sort and filter options.

## Reviving a plant

Changed your mind, or did a comeback happen?

1. Open the resting plant's page from the *Resting* filter
2. Tap 🌱 *Revive*

The plant rejoins your living collection right away — schedule and all, no confirmation needed. It'll start turning up in *Tend* again as soon as its next care is due. 🌱
