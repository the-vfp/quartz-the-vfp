Plant Tracker is a web app that you can install on your phone so it opens from your home screen, runs full-screen, and feels like a native app. Same Plant Tracker, same plants, same care log — just out of your browser tabs and one tap away.

> [!NOTE] What "installing" actually does
> There's nothing to download from an app store. Installing copies a shortcut to your home screen and tells your phone to launch the site full-screen. You can uninstall any time by deleting the icon — your data stays put.

## Installing on iPhone

1. Open Plant Tracker in **Safari** — the install option doesn't appear in Chrome or Firefox on iOS
2. Tap the *Share* button at the bottom of the screen
3. Scroll down and tap *Add to Home Screen*
4. Adjust the name if you'd like, then tap *Add*

The Plant Tracker icon will now appear on your home screen.

## Installing on Android

1. Open Plant Tracker in **Chrome**
2. Tap the *⋮* menu in the top-right corner
3. Tap *Install app* (some Chrome versions label this *Add to Home Screen*)
4. Tap *Install* to confirm

The Plant Tracker icon will now appear on your home screen and in your app drawer.

## Opening the installed app

After install, tap the Plant Tracker icon the same way you'd open any other app. Your plants, photos, and care log are all there waiting — installing the app doesn't move your data, it just changes how you launch the tracker.


> [!warning] Uninstalling or deleting the app
> Your information will still be there if you uninstall or delete the app. However, if you clear your browser's cache, your data will be deleted. Make sure you [[Exporting and importing data|export your data]] regularly or before you clear your cache.
