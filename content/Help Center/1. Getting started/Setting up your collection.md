Welcome! This article is the short path through your first ten minutes with Plant Tracker — installing it, adding a plant, and logging the first watering. By the end, you'll have a real collection and a sense of how the daily rhythm plays out 🌱

Each section below is a teaser pointing to a longer article with screenshots and details. The goal here is to get you oriented, not to explain every button.

![[plant-tracker-expanded-dashboard.png|320]]

## Installing the app

Plant Tracker is a web app, which means there's nothing to download from the App Store or Play Store. You open it in your browser like any website. To make it feel like a proper app, you can install it to your home screen — that gives it its own icon, full-screen mode, and a one-tap launch.

The [[Installing Plant Tracker on your phone|install guide]] walks through the iPhone (Safari) and Android (Chrome) flows. Skip this section if you'd rather use it as a browser tab — everything works the same either way.

## Adding your first plant

Open the home dashboard and head to the *All Plants* tab. Tap *+* in the bottom-right corner to start. You'll be asked for a name (whatever you call this plant — *Agnes*, *Big Boy*, *Plant 1*), a type (the species, like *Maranta Red Prayer Plant*), how often it should be watered, and an icon.

A full walkthrough of the form lives in [[Adding a plant]]. You can change any of these settings later, so don't sweat the details on round one.

## Logging your first watering

Once your plant exists, water it (in real life), then tap the 💧 button on its row to log it. The watering shows up in your *Care Log* with a timestamp, and the tracker starts counting toward the next due day based on the schedule you set.

You can also log from the plant's own page using the bigger 💧 *Water Now* button — see [[Tending to your plants]] for the small differences.

From here on, the daily rhythm is simple: open the dashboard, glance at *Tend*, handle what's due, tap to log it.

## Where to go from here

Once your collection has a few plants in it, these articles dig into the parts you'll use most:

- [[Home dashboard overview]] — what each tab and area on the dashboard is for
- [[Managing notes|Notes]] and [[Managing photos|photos]] — recording what's happening with each plant beyond watering
- [[Setting a plant's care schedule|Setting the care schedule]] and [[Watering Cadence Chart Overview|the cadence chart]] — tuning how often each plant gets watered and tended

When you're ready to protect your work, [[Exporting and importing data|export a backup]]. Plant Tracker stores everything on your phone, so backups are the only way to keep your data when you switch devices or clear your browser. Worth doing before you have a lot to lose.

If something goes sideways, [[Troubleshooting]] and the [[Frequently asked questions|FAQ]] are the first stops.
