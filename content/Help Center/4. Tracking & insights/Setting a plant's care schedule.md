Every plant has a care schedule — the reminders that decide when it turns up needing attention. Watering is always on; fertilizing, rotating, and pest control are yours to switch on per plant. You set them all in one place, measured in days.

> [!TIP] Schedule vs cadence
> The days you set here are your plant's **schedule** — what the tracker uses to _look ahead_ and remind you when care is next due. The [[Watering Cadence Chart Overview|cadence chart]] shows the **cadence** — _looking back_ at how often you've actually watered. They don't change each other; use them together to tune your routine.

## Setting the schedule

![[plant-tracker-care-schedule.png|320]]

1. Open the plant's page
2. Tap *Edit*
3. Under *Care schedule*, set the intervals you want:
	- *Water (days)* — days between waterings; defaults to 7
	- *Fertilize (days)* — leave blank for no reminder
	- *Rotate (days)* — leave blank for no reminder
	- *Pest control (days)* — leave blank for no reminder
4. Tap *Save Changes* ✅

Only watering is required. Leave fertilize, rotate, or pest control blank to skip them — set any to a number and that task starts surfacing when it's due.

## Where the schedule shows up

![[plant-tracker-serena-schedule.png|320]]

Back on the plant's page, a *Care schedule* section lists each task you've turned on, how often it runs, and when it's next due — `Water every 7d · in 2d`, `Fertilize every 30d · 4d overdue`. When a task comes due, it appears in the *Tend* tab on the [[Home dashboard overview|home dashboard]], where one tap marks it done and resets the countdown.

For example, set Agnes to water every 4 days and she'll surface in *Tend* 4 days after you last watered her. Add a 30-day fertilize cycle and she'll reappear for that a month after her last feed — each task keeps its own clock.

> [!NOTE] What counts as "done"
> Each task's countdown reads from your logs: the last watering for water, and the last note carrying that task's emoji (🧪, 🔄, 🐛) for the others. Marking a task done from the *Tend* tab adds that log for you. A task you've never logged shows as *due now* until its first time.
