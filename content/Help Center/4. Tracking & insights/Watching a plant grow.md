Once a plant has a few [[Managing photos|photos]], you can see them laid out as a timeline — a top-to-bottom view of how far it's come. It's the most satisfying screen in Plant Tracker, and it's one tap from the plant's page. 🌱

![[plant-tracker-see-growth.png|320]]

## Opening the growth timeline

1. Open the plant's page
2. Scroll to the photo section
3. Tap 📸 *See growth →*

The button shows up once a plant has **two or more photos** — a single photo isn't a timeline yet, so keep snapping until you've got a before and an after.

## Reading the timeline

![[plant-tracker-growth-timeline.png|320]]

Photos run oldest to newest down the page, each stamped with how long after you got the plant it was taken:

- *Day 1* for the earliest, then *Day 3*, *Day 5* through the first week
- *+3 weeks*, *+5 months* as time stretches on
- *+1.5 years* for the long-haulers

The newest photo wears a *Latest* badge, and every photo shows its actual date too. Tap any one to open it full-screen. The header above sums up the journey — the plant's name, how long you've had it, and the photo count, like *3 months · 4 photos*. Tap the back arrow to return to the plant's page.

> [!NOTE] Stamps count from when you got the plant
> The *Day 1 / +3 weeks* labels are measured from the plant's acquired date — the day you added it to your collection — not from your first photo. So a shot taken the day you brought it home reads *Day 1*, even if you didn't photograph it until later.
