Care logs get long. The emoji filter lets you focus one down to just what you're after — show only waterings, hide photos, pull up every fertilizing you've logged. It works the same on the [[Home dashboard overview|Care Log]] tab on the home dashboard and on the *Care Log* on each plant's page.

![[plant-tracker-care-log-filter.png|320]]

## Where the filter appears

A row of emoji chips sits above the log. Each chip is one kind of entry that's actually in that log — 💧 for waterings, 📷 for photos, and whatever emoji your [[Managing notes|notes]] carry (🧪, ✂️, and so on).

The chips only appear when there are at least two different kinds of entry to sort between. A log that's all waterings has nothing to filter, so the row stays hidden.

## Showing or hiding one kind

Tapping a single chip cycles it through three states:

1. **Off** — the chip sits quiet and everything shows. This is the starting point.
2. **Show only** — one tap; the log narrows to just that kind of entry.
3. **Hide** — a second tap; that kind is hidden and everything else shows.

A third tap returns the chip to off. The chip's tooltip always spells out what the next tap will do, so you're never guessing. One tap on 💧 gives you a watering-only history; a second tap flips it to "everything except waterings."

## Combining chips

Once a second chip joins in, the filter locks to one mode at a time — you're either **showing** a set or **hiding** a set, never mixing the two:

- If you're showing only 💧, tapping 🧪 adds it, so you see waterings *and* fertilizing.
- If you're hiding 📷, tapping 📝 hides notes too.

Tap any active chip again to drop it from the filter. To switch from show-mode to hide-mode (or back), clear down to a single chip first.

> [!TIP] Reset everything
> Tap the *All* chip at the start of the row to clear every filter at once and bring the whole log back.

## On a single plant's page

A plant's own *Care Log* filters in exactly the same way. The one difference: [[Managing notes|pinned notes]] live in their own 📌 *Pinned* group above the log and ignore the filter completely — they stay visible no matter what you've got selected.
