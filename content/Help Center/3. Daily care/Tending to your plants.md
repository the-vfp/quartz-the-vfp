The *Tend* tab is your daily to-do list. Plant Tracker rounds up everything that's come due — waterings first, plus any fertilizing, rotating, or pest control you've put on a [[Setting a plant's care schedule|care schedule]] — and tending a plant is just a matter of checking it off. Watering is the task you'll reach for most, so it gets a couple of extra shortcuts, but it all works the same way underneath.

## Marking what's due done

![[plant-tracker-expanded-dashboard.png|320]]

Every task that's due or overdue gathers in the *Tend* tab, the most overdue at the top. Whatever it is — water, fertilize, rotate, pest control — you clear it the same way:

1. Open the home dashboard — the *Tend* tab is showing by default
2. Find the task you've taken care of
3. Tap the ✓ on the right of its row

The task logs with the current time, its next due date recalculates, and the row drops off the list. Watering records a watering; fertilizing, rotating, and pest control each add a note stamped with the task's emoji (🧪, 🔄, 🐛), so there's nothing to type. Clear the whole list and the tab reads "All tended. 🌿".

> [!NOTE] What counts as done
> Each task reads from your logs — the last watering for water, the last note with the matching emoji for the rest. A task you've never logged shows as *due now* until its first time. [[Setting a plant's care schedule]] covers how the schedule decides what's due.

## Watering a plant

Watering is the everyday one, so beyond the *Tend* tab there are two more one-tap spots — for topping a plant up before it's due, or logging from wherever you already are. Every route records the same thing: a watering stamped with the current time.

### From the All Plants tab

![[plant-tracker-all-plants.png|320]]

1. Open the *All Plants* tab
2. Tap the 💧 on the right of the plant's row

### From the plant's page

![[plant-tracker-agnes-page.png|320]]

1. Open the plant's page from any tab
2. Tap 💧 *Water Now*

Reach for this when you're already on the page — just after a note or photo, say — and don't want to tap back out.

Each watering adds a bar to the plant's [[Watering Cadence Chart Overview|cadence chart]] and nudges the next reminder forward by however many days its [[Setting a plant's care schedule|schedule]] says.

## Undoing a watering

Tapped 💧 by mistake? You can lift a watering back off the log from the plant's page.

> [!WARNING] No confirmation, no undo
> A watering comes off the moment you tap the ×. There's no "are you sure," and no way to bring it back — so be deliberate.

![[plant-tracker-watering-log.png|320]]

1. Open the plant's page
2. Scroll to the *Care Log*
3. Find the watering — the rows with the 💧 icon — and tap the × on the right

The [[Watering Cadence Chart Overview|cadence chart]] adjusts on its own, and the next watering recalculates from your now-newest one.

> [!TIP] Logging anything else
> Repotting, pruning, a fertilizer brand, a passing observation — anything that isn't a due reminder goes in as a note. [[Managing notes]] covers adding, editing, and pinning them.
