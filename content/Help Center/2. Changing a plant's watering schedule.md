You can change how often the tracker will remind you to water a plant! Each plant has a *Water Every (days)* number that you can adjust and it'll affect the next time that the plant appears "Thirsty" in the dashboard. 

For example, if Agnes is set to be watered every 4 days, she'll appear as "Thirsty" in the dashboard 4 days after I last watered her.

> [!TIP] Number of days setting vs cadence graph
>The number of days you set is your plant's **schedule** — it's what the tracker uses to _look ahead_ and remind you when to water next. The [[3. Watering Cadence Chart Overview|cadence graph]] shows the **cadence** — _looking back_ at how frequently you've actually watered. They don't affect each other in the app, but it's a good idea to use them in tandem so that your schedule aligns with how often you're actually watering your plants.

## Changing the watering schedule

![[plant-tracker-agnes-page.png|320]]

1. Navigate to an individual plant's page
2. Tap *Edit*

![[Pasted image plant-tracker-agnes-edit.png|304]]

3. In the *Water Every (days)*, adjust the number as needed
4. Make sure you *Save Changes* ✅
