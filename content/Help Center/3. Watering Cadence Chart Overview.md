The graph measures how many days are in between each watering for a plant — your **cadence**. This way, you can see how often you're actually watering your plant!

Use this information to [[2. Changing a plant's watering schedule|adjust the watering cadence]] you've set for your plant in the app, to note any changes to its watering needs if any conditions have changed, or to congratulate yourself on a plant well watered 🥂

![[plant-tracker-agnes-expanded-graph.png|320]]

## Viewing the chart

You can view the watering cadence graph by opening a plant's page and then tapping on *Watering Cadence* right below the *Water Now* and *Add Photo* buttons. Tap on *Watering Cadence* again to hide the graph.

## Reading the chart

Every bar in the graph tells you how many days passed between one watering and the next. You can set the range you'd like to see the watering cadence for — last 10, 20, or all days since its first watering — and the text right above the graph will tell you the average number of days between waterings the plant had over that range.

## How the chart updates

This graph is based on waterings that have been logged, so if you delete or add watering events, it'll automatically update.
