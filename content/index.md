<div class="hp-eyebrow">03 · Cold Storage</div>
<h3 class="hp-proj-title">Cold Storage</h3>
<div class="hp-proj-sub">A campaign wiki built from primary sources</div>
<p class="hp-proj-body">A wiki for a Vampire: The Masquerade chronicle — and a workflow experiment. I recorded our game sessions, then used the auto-generated captions as source material for AI-drafted documentation. The question I wanted to answer was “can you go from primary source to structured, cross-referenced knowledge without writing every word by hand?”</p>
